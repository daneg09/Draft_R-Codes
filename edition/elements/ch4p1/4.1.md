## Merit assessment `M4.1`: Site area merit assessment criteria

M4.1.1 Development of the type and density indicated by the density code designated in the scheme. M4.1.2 The WAPC, in consultation with the local government, may approve the creation of a green title lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot with an average site area up to 5 per cent less than that specified in Table 4B where it will: i. facilitate the protection of an environmental or heritage feature; ii. facilitate the retention of a significant element that contributes toward an existing streetscape worthy of retention; iii. facilitate the development of lots with separate and sufficient frontage to more than one public street; iv. overcome a special or unusual limitation on the development of the land imposed by its size, shape or other feature; v. allow land to be developed with housing of the same type and form as land in the vicinity and which would not otherwise be able to be developed; or vi. achieve specific objectives of the local planning framework. [Potential change: Local governments to have ability to approve variations to minimum and/or average site area up to 5 per cent through a development approval.] M4.1.3 Notwithstanding M4.1.2, for apartments in areas coded R40 - R60; the decision-maker may approve a development application involving the creation of a strata lot of a lesser average site area than that specified in Table 4B provided that the proposed variation does not limit the ability for the development to achieve the relevant provisions of the R-Codes, and will: i. facilitate housing and dwelling diversity; ii. facilitate the protection of an environmental or heritage feature; iii. facilitate the retention of a significant element that contributes toward an existing streetscape worthy of retention; iv. allow land to be developed with housing of the same types and form as land in the vicinity and which would not otherwise be able to be developed; or v. achieve specific objectives of the local planning framework. M4.1.4 The WAPC, in consultation with the local government, may approve the creation of a survey-strata lot or strata lot for an existing authorised grouped dwelling or apartment development of a lesser minimum and average site area than that specified in Table 4B, where, in the opinion of the WAPC or the local government, the development on the resulting survey-strata or strata lots is consistent with the objectives of the relevant design elements of the R-Codes, and the orderly and proper planning of the locality.

Source p49.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `site.area_m2`: What is the area? (m²; site area)
- `subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2`: What is the truncation area added? (m²; C4.1.2 (max 20 m2))
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `site.r_code`: What is the r code? (code; R-coding of the lot)
- `site.lot_approved_by_wapc`: The lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot was approved by the WAPC at its current area (C4.1.4(i))? (yes/no)
- `site.existing_lot_with_legal_access`: Existing lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot with permanent legal access to a public road (C4.1.4(ii))? (yes/no)
- `proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count`: What is the reduced site area dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings whose site area relies on the C4.1.5 to C4.1.7 reduction (C4.1.6(ii), C4.1.7(ii) 50% caps))
- `proposal.reduced_small_dwelling_count`: What is the reduced small dwelling? (of proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count, how many are small dwellings (C2.9.1); C4.1.4 and the C4.1.6 50% cap count small dwellings only (C4.1.1))
- `site.significant_tree_retained`: A significant existing tree is retained (C1.2.4)? (yes/no)

### `C4.1.1` Average site area per apartment (Table 4B) ⚑

Facts: `site.area_m2`, `subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2`, `proposal.dwelling_count`, `site.r_code`, `site.lot_approved_by_wapc`, `site.existing_lot_with_legal_access`, `proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count`, `proposal.reduced_small_dwelling_count`, `site.significant_tree_retained`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p49 Table 4B.

> Minimum and average site area. C4.1.1 Development which complies with the dwelling type and site area provisions set out in Table 4B and the following provisions. Table 4B Site area requirements. Site area per dwelling (m2): R10 Av 1000; R12.5 Av 800; R15 Av 666; R17.5 Av 571; R20 Av 450; R25 Av 350; R30 Av 300; R35 Av 260; R40 Av 115; R50 Av 100; R60 Av 85. Site area variations. Site area variations are subject to C4.1.1. C4.1.3 The following variations to the minimum and average site area set out in Table 4B may be made: i. in the case of a apartments; the area of a lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot approved by the WAPC; or ii. the area of any existing lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot with permanent legal access to a public road, notwithstanding that it is less than that required in Table 4B. C4.1.4 In areas coded R25 and below; for a small dwelling (in accordance with C2.9.1) that is the subject of a proposed development, the minimum and average site area of Table 4B may be reduced by up to 35 per cent. C4.1.5 In areas coded R30 to R40; for an accessible dwelling designed to gold level universal design (in accordance with C2.7.1), or a small dwelling (in accordance with C2.9.1) that is the subject of a development proposal, the minimum and average site area of Table 4B may be reduced by up to 35 per cent, provided that: i. for development or subdivision of 4 or more dwellings or sites, the site area reduction is limited to a maximum 50 per cent of the total number of dwellings or sites. C4.1.6 In areas coded R50 and above; for an accessible dwelling designed to gold level universal design (in accordance with C2.7.1), or a small dwelling (in accordance with C2.9.1) that is the subject of a development proposal, the minimum and average site area of Table 4B may be reduced by up to 35 per cent, provided that: i. the site area reduction is limited for small dwellings to a maximum 50 per cent of the total number of dwellings or sites. C4.1.7 For apartments in areas coded R30 to R60; where a significant existing tree is retained on a site that is subject to a development proposal, the average site area of Table 4B may be reduced by 10 per cent. This reduction is limited to dwellings not already subject to a reduced average site area under C4.1.3.

Test: (site.area_m2 + min(subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2, 20)) / proposal.dwelling_count >= Table 4B-avg by coding.
- Table 4B-avg (Table 4B Average site area per dwelling (apartments)): R10: 1000 m2; R12.5: 800 m2; R15: 666 m2; R17.5: 571 m2; R20: 450 m2; R25: 350 m2; R30: 300 m2; R35: 260 m2; R40: 115 m2; R50: 100 m2; R60: 85 m2.
Exception 1: C4.1.3(i) area of a lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot approved by the WAPC. When site.lot_approved_by_wapc = yes: the provision does not apply. (C4.1.3(i) (register C4P1-142)) ⚑
Exception 2: C4.1.3(ii) existing lot with permanent legal access to a public road. When site.existing_lot_with_legal_access = yes: the provision does not apply. (C4.1.3(ii) (register C4P1-142)) ⚑
Exception 3: C4.1.4 small dwellings at R25 and below: average site area reduced by up to 35% per small dwelling. When (Table 4.1a by coding gives yes AND proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count >= 1 AND proposal.reduced_small_dwelling_count == proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count): test becomes: (site.area_m2 + min(subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2, 20)) / (proposal.dwelling_count - 0.35 * proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count) >= Table 4B-avg by coding. (C4.1.4 (register C4P1-143))
Exception 4: C4.1.5 gold-level accessible or small dwellings at R30 to R40: reduced by up to 35%, at most 50% of 4 or more dwellings. When (Table 4.1b by coding gives yes AND proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count >= 1): test becomes: ((site.area_m2 + min(subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2, 20)) / (proposal.dwelling_count - 0.35 * proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count) >= Table 4B-avg by coding AND (proposal.dwelling_count < 4 OR proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count as % of proposal.dwelling_count <= 50%)). (C4.1.5 (register C4P1-144))
Exception 5: C4.1.6 gold-level accessible or small dwellings at R50 and above: reduced by up to 35%, small dwellings at most 50% of dwellings. When (Table 4.1c by coding gives yes AND proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count >= 1): test becomes: ((site.area_m2 + min(subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2, 20)) / (proposal.dwelling_count - 0.35 * proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count) >= Table 4B-avg by coding AND proposal.reduced_small_dwelling_count as % of proposal.dwelling_count <= 50%). (C4.1.6 (register C4P1-145))
Exception 6: C4.1.7 significant existing tree retained at R30 to R60: average site area reduced by 10% for dwellings not otherwise reduced. When (Table 4.1d by coding gives yes AND site.significant_tree_retained = yes): test becomes: ((site.area_m2 + min(subdivision.truncation_area_added_m2, 20)) / (0.9 * (proposal.dwelling_count - proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count) + 0.65 * proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count) >= Table 4B-avg by coding AND (proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count <= 0 OR (Table 4.1b by coding gives yes AND (proposal.dwelling_count < 4 OR proposal.reduced_site_area_dwelling_count as % of proposal.dwelling_count <= 50%)) OR (Table 4.1c by coding gives yes AND proposal.reduced_small_dwelling_count as % of proposal.dwelling_count <= 50%))). (C4.1.7 (register C4P1-146)) ⚑
Not met: assess against `M4.1`.
Defined terms:
- *site area* (A1 p9): the area of land required for the construction of a dwelling to satisfy the requirements of the R-Codes.
- *small dwelling* (A1 p9): a single house or grouped dwelling with an internal floor area no greater that 70m²; or a multiple dwelling with an internal floor area no greater than 60m².
- *significant existing tree* (A1 p9): an existing tree that meets the following criteria: (i) healthy specimens with ongoing viability; and (ii) species is not included on a State or local area weed register; and (iii) height of at least 4m; and/or (iv) trunk diameter of at least 160mm, measured 1m from the ground; and/or (v) average canopy diameter of at least 4m; or (vi) as specified with the local planning framework.
- *survey-strata lot* (A1 p10): refer to definition for lot in a survey-strata scheme.
- *strata lot* (A1 p10): refer to definition for a lot in a strata scheme.
⚑ Verification note: C4.1.7 (p50) limits the 10% to dwellings 'not already subject to a reduced average site area under C4.1.3'; C4.1.3 is the WAPC/existing-lot variation and the concessions are C4.1.4-C4.1.6 (cross-reference error on the page). C4.1.3(i) 'in the case of a apartments; the area of a lot ... approved by the WAPC' and C4.1.3(ii) (any existing lot with legal road access) read literally waive Table 4B. C4.1.3-C4.1.6 refer to 'the minimum and average site area of Table 4B' but Table 4B (p51) prints an average column only. Table 4B values and the 35%, 50% and 10% concessions confirmed.
