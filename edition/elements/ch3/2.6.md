## Merit assessment `M2.6`: Outbuildings merit assessment criteria

M2.6.1 Outbuildings minimise negative impacts on the amenity for residents of the development, adjoining properties or the streetscape. M2.6.2 Siting and size of outbuildings does not compromise the ability to provide adequate primary garden area, soft landscaping, trees and deep soil areas.

Source p22.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `outbuilding.present`: Present? (yes/no)
- `outbuilding.total_area_m2`: What is the total area? (m²; individual or collective area per site)
- `outbuilding.ridge_height_m`: What is the ridge height? (m)
- `outbuilding.in_street_setback_area`: Primary or secondary street setback area? (yes/no)
- `outbuilding.wall_height_m`: What is the wall height? (m)
- `outbuilding.matches_dwelling_materials`: External wall and roof materials and colour match (C2.6.2)? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C2.6.1` Outbuilding area, ridge height and location per Table 2.6a

Facts: `outbuilding.present`, `outbuilding.total_area_m2`, `outbuilding.ridge_height_m`, `outbuilding.in_street_setback_area`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p22 Table 2.6a.

> C2.6.1 Any outbuilding is provided in accordance with Table 2.6a, provided that it: i. is set back or built up to lot boundaries in accordance with C3.4.1, C3.4.4, C3.4.5 or C3.4.6; ii. does not exceed the maximum allowable site cover in accordance with C3.1.1; iii. does not reduce the minimum outdoor living area or primary garden area required in accordance with C1.1.1 or C1.1.3; iv. does not reduce the minimum soft landscaping required in accordance with C1.2.1; and v. does not reduce the minimum tree provision and associated deep soil area in accordance with C1.2.4. Table 2.6a Outbuilding provisions: maximum individual or collective area per site 60m2; maximum wall height 3m; maximum ridge height 4.2m; location: not located within the primary or secondary street setback area. Refer to Figure 2.6a for wall and ridge height guidance.

Test: (outbuilding.total_area_m2 <= 60.0 m² AND outbuilding.ridge_height_m <= 4.2 m AND outbuilding.in_street_setback_area = no).
Applies only when: outbuilding.present = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.6`.
Defined terms:
- *outbuilding* (A1 p8): an enclosed non-habitable structure that is detached from any dwelling and may include a detached garage.

### `C2.6.1-r1` Outbuilding wall height (3 m, or 3.5 m where materials match the dwelling) ⚑

Facts: `outbuilding.present`, `outbuilding.wall_height_m`, `outbuilding.matches_dwelling_materials`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p22 Table 2.6a.

> Table 2.6a maximum wall height 3m. C2.6.2 Notwithstanding C2.6.1(i), an outbuilding can have a wall height of up to 3.5m where the external wall and roof materials and finishes (including colour) are the same as those of the dwelling on the same site. Outbuildings will need to comply with the NCC requirements, including but not limited to fire separation.

Test: outbuilding.wall_height_m <= 3.0 m.
Applies only when: outbuilding.present = yes.
Exception 1: C2.6.2: external wall and roof materials, finishes and colour match the dwelling. When outbuilding.matches_dwelling_materials = yes: sets value = 3.5. (C2.6.2, p21) ⚑
Not met: assess against `M2.6`.
Defined terms:
- *height, wall* (A1 p6): this is the vertical distance between the point where the base of the wall meets the natural ground level at the boundary immediately adjacent to the wall and measured to the top of the wall or parapet.
⚑ Verification note: Values (3m, 3.5m) match p22. Ambiguous on the page: C2.6.2 says 'Notwithstanding C2.6.1(i)', but C2.6.1(i) is the lot boundary setback cross-reference, not the wall height in Table 2.6a.
