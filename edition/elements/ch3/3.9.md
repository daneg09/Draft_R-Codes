## Merit assessment `M3.9`: Solar access for adjoining sites merit assessment criteria

M3.9.1 Development is designed to protect solar access for neighbouring sites taking into account the potential to overshadow: i. major openings to primary living spaces, in particular those oriented between north west, north and east; ii. private open space and communal open space; and iii. roof mounted solar collectors.

Source p44.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `adjoining.south.r_code`: What is the south r code? (code; coding of the lot that receives the shadow)
- `building.max_wall_height_m`: What is the max wall height? (m; highest wall (for the single-storey overshadowing exemption))
- `building.storeys`: What is the storeys?
- `overshadow.adjoining_pct`: What is the adjoining? (%; shadow cast on the adjoining property as % of its site area)
- `overshadow.adjoining_rear_half_pct`: What is the adjoining rear half? (%; shadow on the rear half of a narrow adjoining lot (Table 3.9b))
- `site.climate_zone`: What is the climate zone? (NCC climate zone (4, 5, 6 trigger overshadowing))
- `adjoining.diagonal.r_code`: What is the diagonal r code? (code; diagonally adjacent lot receiving shadow)
- `overshadow.diagonal_pct`: What is the diagonal? (%; on the diagonally adjacent lot)
- `adjoining.south.battleaxe`: The adjoining southern lot is a battleaxe lot (C3.9.3(ii))? (yes/no)
- `adjoining.south.frontage_m`: What is the south frontage? (m)
- `adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count`: What is the north shared boundary? (number of lots sharing the northern boundary (C3.9.4))
- `overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary`: C3.9.4? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C3.9.1` Maximum overshadowing of an adjoining property (Table 3.9a)

Facts: `adjoining.south.r_code`, `building.max_wall_height_m`, `building.storeys`, `overshadow.adjoining_pct`, `overshadow.adjoining_rear_half_pct`, `site.climate_zone`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding numeric,LR,MR,HR,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p44 Table 3.9a.

> In climate zones 4, 5, and 6, development is designed that its shadow cast at midday, 21 June (refer Figure 3.9a) onto any other adjoining property and/or diagonally adjacent lot (refer Figure 3.9b) does not exceed the limits set out in Table 3.9a.

Test: overshadow.adjoining_pct <= Table 3.9a by coding.
- Table 3.9a (Table 3.9a Maximum overshadowing: adjoining property (percentage of dwelling site area)): R25-: 25 % (R25 and lower); R30-R40: 35 %; R50-R60: 50 %; R80+: no provision (R80 or higher: no maximum overshadowing).
Applies only when: (site.climate_zone >= 4 AND site.climate_zone <= 6).
Exception 1: C3.9.2 single storey, wall height not more than 3.5 m. When (building.storeys == 1 AND building.max_wall_height_m <= 3.5 m): the provision does not apply. (C3.9.2, p43) ⚑
Exception 2: C3.9.3 narrow adjoining lot (R40+, frontage 7.5 m or less): Table 3.9b governs. When overshadow.adjoining_rear_half_pct <= Table 3.9b by coding,frontage_m: the provision does not apply. (C3.9.3, p43) ⚑
Not met: assess against `M3.9`.
Defined terms:
- *adjoining property* (A1 p4): any lot: (i) on which any dwelling for which provision is made in the R-Codes may be constructed under the scheme; and (ii) which shares a boundary or portion of a boundary with a lot on which there is a proposed residential development site or is separated from that lot by a right-of-way, vehicle access way, pedestrian access way, access leg of a battleaxe lot or the equivalent not more than 6m in width.
- *site area* (A1 p9): the area of land required for the construction of a dwelling to satisfy the requirements of the R-Codes.
- *natural ground level* (A1 p7): the levels on a site which precede the proposed development, excluding any site works unless approved by the decision-maker or established as part of subdivision of the land preceding development.

### `C3.9.3` Overshadowing of a narrow adjoining lot (Table 3.9b, rear half of site)

Facts: `adjoining.south.battleaxe`, `adjoining.south.frontage_m`, `adjoining.south.r_code`, `overshadow.adjoining_rear_half_pct`, `site.climate_zone`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding numeric,LR,MR,HR,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p45 Table 3.9b.

> Notwithstanding C3.9.1, in climate zones 4, 5, and 6, where the adjoining property is: i. coded R40 or greater; and ii. has a lot frontage 7.5m or less (excluding battleaxe lots); iii. development is designed so that its shadow cast at midday, 21 June onto any other adjoining property does not exceed the limits set out in Table 3.9b.

Test: overshadow.adjoining_rear_half_pct <= Table 3.9b by coding,frontage_m.
- Table 3.9b (Table 3.9b Maximum overshadowing for narrow lots (rear half of the adjoining site)): R40, frontage_m <= 7.5: 35 % (no maximum on the front half; 35% of the rear half); R50-R60, frontage_m <= 7.5: 55 % (no maximum on the front half; 55% of the rear half); R80+, frontage_m <= 7.5: no provision (R80 and higher: no maximum overshadowing).
Applies only when: (site.climate_zone >= 4 AND site.climate_zone <= 6); adjoining.south.battleaxe = no.
Not met: assess against `M3.9`.
Defined terms:
- *frontage* (A1 p5): the width of a lot at the primary street setback line, provided that in the case of battleaxe or other irregularly shaped lots, it shall be as determined by the decision-maker.
- *battleaxe lot* (A1 p4): a single house lot that has a frontage for purposes of servicing and access to a public road only through a strip of connecting land containing a pedestrian access leg and/ or vehicular access way that is part of the lot. The term excludes a site that has vehicle access from a private or communal street or right-of-way connected to a public road.

### `C3.9.4` Proportional shading limit where the neighbour's northern boundary is shared by several lots

Facts: `adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count`, `overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary`, `site.climate_zone`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding numeric,LR,MR,HR,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p45.

> Where an adjoining property shares a northern lot boundary with more than one lot including the development site, the limit of shading at C3.9.1 shall be cumulative and proportional to the length of the shared boundary/ies of the development site (refer Figure 3.9f). This does not apply to diagonally adjacent lots.

Test: overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary = yes.
Applies only when: (site.climate_zone >= 4 AND site.climate_zone <= 6); adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count >= 2.
Not met: assess against `M3.9`.
Defined terms:
- *lot boundary* (A1 p7): the boundary between a lot and any other parcel of land, excluding a street boundary.
