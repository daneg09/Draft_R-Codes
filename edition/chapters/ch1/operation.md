## Figure 2.1 R-Codes Chapter 2, Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 Part 1 approval process

`rcodes:draft-2026-10-24/ch1/F2.1`, clause 2.1.1, p6. When a development application (DA) is required.

| Dwelling type | DA required when all deemed exempt provisions are met |
|---|---|
| single house | no, unless the scheme requires one |
| grouped dwelling | yes |
| multiple dwelling | yes |

- single house: "Proposal meets deemed exempt provisions of the R-Codes and the Scheme -> Yes -> Single house (note 1) -> Development approval not required -> Proceed to building permit. Note 1: A scheme may require development approval for a single house irrespective of the requirements of the R-Codes." Figure 2.1: single house, DA not required where all deemed exempt provisions are met. A scheme may require development approval for a single house irrespective of the R-Codes (heritage, special control areas, estate scheme provisions).
- grouped dwelling: "Proposal meets deemed exempt provisions of the R-Codes and the Scheme -> Yes -> All other residential development -> Development approval required -> Development approval granted." Figure 2.1: all other residential development requires a DA even where all deemed exempt provisions are met (CH1-15).
- multiple dwelling: "Proposal meets deemed exempt provisions of the R-Codes and the Scheme -> Yes -> All other residential development -> Development approval required -> Development approval granted." As for grouped dwellings. Figure 2.1 covers Chapter 4 Part 1 only; Chapter 4 Part 2 approval is not shown in the figure.
- Merit: "Proposal addresses one or more merit assessment criteria of the R-Codes -> Requires judgement of merit; Development approval required (note 1: refer cl. 60 and 61 of part 7 in Schedule 2 of the Regulations)."
- Basis: Clause 61 of Schedule 2 of the Planning and Development (Local Planning Schemes) Regulations 2015 and the local planning scheme. The R-Codes do not themselves exempt development from approval; clause 2.1.3 construes 'deemed exempt' as 'deemed-to-comply' for the purpose of clause 1 of Schedule 2.
