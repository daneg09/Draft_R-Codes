## Merit assessment `M2.1`: Size and layout of dwellings merit assessment criteria

M2.1.1 Dwellings have a primary living space that: i. is proportionate to the type and size of the dwelling and intended number of occupants; ii. has a physical and visual relationship with the primary garden area, private open space and/or public open space; and iii. incorporates environmental design principles, including passive solar design and appropriate daylighting and shading, appropriate for the climate. M2.1.2 Ceiling heights and room dimensions provide for well-proportioned spaces that facilitate good natural ventilation and daylighting. M2.1.3 The size and layout of a dwellings: i. is functional with the ability to flexibly accommodate furniture; ii. is appropriate to the intended number of occupants; and iii. ensures functional, high amenity spaces. M2.1.4 Development provides a mix of dwelling types, sizes and configurations that cater for diverse household types and changing community demographics, appropriate to the existing and/or future context of the locality. M2.1.5 Each dwelling provides adequate, conveniently located storage for large items that are: i. proportionate to the size of the dwelling and intended number of occupants; and ii. integrated into the design of the building and/or screened from view to ensure that it is not visually intrusive when viewed from the street. M2.1.6 The siting and layout of dwellings minimizes potential impacts on amenity and provide appropriate visual and acoustic privacy to habitable rooms by: i. locating, orienting or setting back habitable rooms; ii. providing adequate landscape screening as a buffer; and/or iii. providing acoustic treatments to reduce noise transfer.

Source p15.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `dwellings.min.primary_living_min_dim_m`: What is the min primary living min dim? (m; smallest primary living space dimension (C2.1.1, 3.8 m))
- `dwellings.primary_living_direct_access_to_pos`: C2.1.2? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.max.single_aspect_living_depth_ratio`: What is the max single aspect living depth ratio? (depth of a single-aspect living space divided by ceiling height (C2.1.3, max 3))
- `dwellings.min.bedroom_area_m2`: What is the min bedroom area? (m²; C2.1.4 (9 m2))
- `dwellings.min.bedroom_min_dim_m`: What is the min bedroom min dim? (m; C2.1.4 (2.7 m))
- `dwellings.min.ceiling_height_habitable_m`: What is the min ceiling height habitable? (m; C2.1.5 (2.65 m))
- `dwellings.min.ceiling_height_non_habitable_m`: What is the min ceiling height non habitable? (m; C2.1.5 (2.4 m))
- `dwellings.min.internal_floor_area_m2`: What is the min internal floor area? (m²; smallest apartment against Table 2.1a by bedroom count)
- `dwellings.min.bedrooms_of_smallest`: What is the min bedrooms of smallest? (bedroom count of that apartment)
- `dwellings.min.bathrooms_of_smallest`: What is the min bathrooms of smallest? (bathrooms in the apartment tested for C2.1.6 (Table 2.1a 1-bath / 2-bath rows; +5 m2 per additional bathroom))
- `dwellings.min.separate_toilets_of_smallest`: What is the min separate toilets of smallest? (additional separate toilets in that apartment (+3 m2 each, Table 2.1a note); 0 where none)
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `dwellings.max_same_bedroom_count_pct`: What is the max same bedroom count? (%; share of dwellings with the same bedroom count (C2.1.7, 80%))
- `dwellings.min.storage_area_m2`: What is the min storage area? (m²; Table 2.1b)
- `dwellings.min.storage_bedrooms`: What is the min storage bedrooms? (bedroom count of the apartment with the least compliant storage; key for Table 2.1b)
- `dwellings.min.storage_min_dim_m`: What is the min storage min dim? (m)
- `dwellings.min.storage_height_m`: What is the min storage height? (m; lowest storage area height (Table 2.1b, 2.1 m))
- `dwellings.storage_behind_street_setback`: Every apartment's storage is behind the primary street setback (C2.1.8)? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.storage_opening_not_inward`: Every storage area is accessed via an opening that does not open inwards (C2.1.8)? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.min.storage_circulation_m`: What is the min storage circulation? (m; adjacent circulation where dimension reduced to 1 m (C2.1.9, 0.9 m))
- `dwellings.storage_door_on_greater_dim_80pct_openable`: Where a storage dimension is under 1.5 m, the door is on the greater dimension and openable for at least 80% of its length (C2.1.9(ii))? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.ground_floor_openings_to_car_park`: C2.1.10? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.min.ground_floor_opening_to_car_park_setback_m`: What is the min ground floor opening to car park setback? (m; least setback of a major opening of a ground-floor apartment facing directly onto car parking or a non-residential component (C2.1.10, 3 m))
- `dwellings.min.noise_source_to_habitable_wall_m`: What is the min noise source to habitable wall? (m; C2.1.11 (1 m))
- `dwellings.min.noise_source_to_bedroom_window_m`: What is the min noise source to bedroom window? (m; C2.1.11 (3 m))

## Provisions

### `C2.1.1` Primary living space can accommodate 3.8 m x 3.8 m (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.min.primary_living_min_dim_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p15.

> Each dwelling is to have one room that is the designated primary living space and for development in areas coded R30 to R60, this primary living space can accommodate a dimension of at least 3.8m x 3.8m¹ (refer Figure 2.1a). ¹Exclusive of built-in cabinetry along walls.

Test: dwellings.min.primary_living_min_dim_m >= 3.8 m.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *primary living space* (A1 p8): the identified room(s) within a dwelling that is the focus of life and activity and usually the largest room. This area is connected with private open space and may include any of the following room types: living room, lounge room, family room, or an integrated open plan living area that has one of these room types together with a kitchen or dining area.

### `C2.1.2` Primary living space has direct physical and visual access to private open space (R30-R60) ⚑

Facts: `dwellings.primary_living_direct_access_to_pos`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p15.

> The primary living space is to have direct physical and visual access to a private open space in accordance with Table 2.1a.

Test: dwellings.primary_living_direct_access_to_pos = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *private open space* (A1 p8): means outdoor space located at ground level or on a structure that is within private ownership and provided for the recreational use of residents of the dwelling to which it abuts and excludes car parking spaces and access ways. It includes primary garden areas, outdoor living areas, balconies, courtyards and terraces.
⚑ Verification note: C2.1.2 cites 'Table 2.1a', which on p16 is the minimum dwelling size table, not a private open space table; only the access test is encoded. Heading 'Primary living space (applies to R30-R60 only)' confirmed to cover C2.1.1 to C2.1.3 (p15).

### `C2.1.3` Single-aspect primary living space depth at most 3 x ceiling height (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.max.single_aspect_living_depth_ratio`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p15.

> The maximum depth¹ of a single aspect primary living space shall be a maximum three times (3x) the ceiling height (refer Figure 2.1b). ¹Exclusive of built-in cabinetry along walls. Note: Additional livings spaces (such as a second lounge room) are not subject to the provisions of C2.1.1 – C2.1.3

Test: dwellings.max.single_aspect_living_depth_ratio <= 3.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.

### `C2.1.4` Bedrooms at least 9 m2 and 2.7 m x 2.7 m (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.min.bedroom_area_m2`, `dwellings.min.bedroom_min_dim_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p16.

> Bedrooms have a minimum internal floor area of 9m2 and can accommodate a minimum dimension of 2.7m x 2.7m (refer Figure 2.1c). Minimum area is inclusive of built-in robes and cabinetry, however the minimum dimension excludes built-in robes and cabinetry.

Test: (dwellings.min.bedroom_area_m2 >= 9.0 m² AND dwellings.min.bedroom_min_dim_m >= 2.7 m).
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *internal floor area* (A1 p6): in relation to a room or dwelling, the internal area measured within the finished surfaces of the walls, and includes the area occupied by any cupboard or other built-in furniture, fixture or fitting but excludes any garages.

### `C2.1.5` Ceiling heights at least 2.65 m (habitable) and 2.4 m (non-habitable) (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.min.ceiling_height_habitable_m`, `dwellings.min.ceiling_height_non_habitable_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p16.

> Measured from the finished floor level to the ceiling level, minimum ceiling heights are: i. 2.65m for habitable rooms; and ii. 2.4m for non-habitable rooms. All other ceilings are to meet the requirements of the NCC.

Test: (dwellings.min.ceiling_height_habitable_m >= 2.65 m AND dwellings.min.ceiling_height_non_habitable_m >= 2.4 m).
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *habitable room/space* (A1 p6): as defined by the NCC for a room/space used for normal domestic activities that includes: (i) a bedroom, living room, lounge room, music room, sitting room, television room, kitchen, scullery, dining room, sewing room, study, playroom, family room, sunroom, gymnasium, fully enclosed swimming pool or patio; but excludes: (i) a bathroom, laundry, water closet, pantry, walk-in wardrobe, corridor, hallway, lobby, photographic darkroom, clothes drying room, verandah and unenclosed swimming pool or patio and other spaces of a specialised nature occupied neither frequently nor for extended periods.
- *non-habitable room/space* (A1 p7): any room or space that is not defined as a habitable room or space in the NCC.

### `C2.1.6` Minimum internal floor area per Table 2.1a, plus 3 m2 per extra toilet and 5 m2 per extra bathroom (R30-R60) ⚑

Facts: `dwellings.min.internal_floor_area_m2`, `dwellings.min.bedrooms_of_smallest`, `dwellings.min.bathrooms_of_smallest`, `dwellings.min.separate_toilets_of_smallest`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p16 Table 2.1a.

> Dwellings are to provide minimum internal floor areas in accordance with Table 2.1a. Table 2.1a Minimum dwelling size provisions (Dwelling size: minimum internal floor area): Studio 37m2; 1 bed 47m2; 2 bed x 1 bath 67m2; 2 bed x 2 bath 72m2; 3 bed x 1 bath 90m2; 3 bed x 2 bath 95m2. An additional 3m2 shall be provided for designs that include an additional separate toilet, and 5m2 for designs that include an additional bathroom. Minimum internal floor area excludes storerooms where they are accessed external to the dwelling.

Test: dwellings.min.internal_floor_area_m2 >= (37 if dwellings.min.bedrooms_of_smallest == 0 else 47 if dwellings.min.bedrooms_of_smallest == 1 else 67 if dwellings.min.bedrooms_of_smallest == 2 else 90) + 5 * max(0, dwellings.min.bathrooms_of_smallest - 1) + 3 * dwellings.min.separate_toilets_of_smallest.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *dwelling* (A1 p5): a building or portion of a building being used, adapted, or designed or intended to be used for the purpose of human habitation on a permanent basis by a single person, a single family, or no more than six persons who do not comprise a single family.
⚑ Verification note: Table 2.1a (p16) prints no 4+ bedroom row (read as 3 bed), and its unmarked table note does not say whether the +5 m2 additional bathroom applies to a studio or 1 bed, or whether it stacks on the printed 2x2 and 3x2 rows (read as reproducing them).

### `C2.1.7` More than 10 apartments: no more than 80% with the same number of bedrooms (R30-R60)

Facts: `proposal.dwelling_count`, `dwellings.max_same_bedroom_count_pct`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p16.

> Where more than 10 apartments are proposed, no more than 80 per cent of dwellings have the same number of bedrooms.

Test: dwellings.max_same_bedroom_count_pct <= 80%.
Applies only when: proposal.dwelling_count > 10.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.

### `C2.1.8` Dedicated weatherproof storage per Table 2.1b, behind the primary street setback, opening not inwards

Facts: `dwellings.min.storage_area_m2`, `dwellings.min.storage_bedrooms`, `dwellings.min.storage_min_dim_m`, `dwellings.min.storage_height_m`, `dwellings.storage_behind_street_setback`, `dwellings.storage_opening_not_inward`, `dwellings.min.storage_circulation_m`, `dwellings.storage_door_on_greater_dim_80pct_openable`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p17 Table 2.1b.

> Each dwelling has exclusive use of a dedicated, weatherproof storage area in accordance with Table 2.1b, that is located behind the primary street setback and accessible via an opening that does not open inwards. C2.1.9 Notwithstanding C2.1.8, minimum storage area dimension can be reduced to 1m where: i. it can be demonstrated that an adjacent circulation space achieves 0.9m clearance; ii. the door or opening is located on the greater dimension and is openable for a minimum 80 per cent of the length; and iii. the minimum storage area is still achieved (refer Figure 2.1d). Table 2.1b Storage provisions: Studio / 1 bedroom dwelling 3m2; 2 bedroom dwelling 4m2; 3+ bed bedroom dwelling 5m2; minimum storage area dimension¹ 1.5m; minimum storage area height 2.1m; location behind the primary street setback. Minimum dimension refers to the minimum length and width of the storage area. Storage can be co-located within a garage or carport but must provide a dedicated area. Dimensions and areas are exclusive of services, plant, utilities, bin storage, bicycle parking and fixtures and facilities. ¹Minimum dimension can be reduced in accordance with C2.1.9

Test: (dwellings.min.storage_area_m2 >= Table 2.1b by storage_bedrooms AND dwellings.min.storage_min_dim_m >= 1.5 m AND dwellings.min.storage_height_m >= 2.1 m AND dwellings.storage_behind_street_setback = yes AND dwellings.storage_opening_not_inward = yes).
- Table 2.1b (Table 2.1b Storage provisions (minimum storage area)): storage_bedrooms <= 1: 3 m2 (studio / 1 bedroom dwelling); storage_bedrooms 2-2: 4 m2 (2 bedroom dwelling); storage_bedrooms >= 3: 5 m2 (3+ bedroom dwelling).
Exception 1: C2.1.9: minimum dimension reduced to 1 m with 0.9 m adjacent circulation and the door on the greater dimension, openable for 80% of its length. When (dwellings.min.storage_circulation_m >= 0.9 m AND dwellings.storage_door_on_greater_dim_80pct_openable = yes): test becomes: (dwellings.min.storage_area_m2 >= Table 2.1b by storage_bedrooms AND dwellings.min.storage_min_dim_m >= 1.0 m AND dwellings.min.storage_height_m >= 2.1 m AND dwellings.storage_behind_street_setback = yes AND dwellings.storage_opening_not_inward = yes). (C2.1.9, p17 (register C4P1-030))
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.

### `C2.1.10` Major openings of ground-floor apartments facing car parking or non-residential uses set back at least 3 m (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.ground_floor_openings_to_car_park`, `dwellings.min.ground_floor_opening_to_car_park_setback_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p17.

> Major openings to ground floor apartments facing directly onto car parking areas and/or non-residential components of a mixed use development are set back a minimum 3m and are designed to ensure visual privacy and manage noise intrusion and light spill.

Test: dwellings.min.ground_floor_opening_to_car_park_setback_m >= 3.0 m.
Applies only when: dwellings.ground_floor_openings_to_car_park = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *major opening* (A1 p7): a window, door or other opening in the exterior wall of a habitable room that provides external means of light or view for that room or space, but does not include an opening or openings that: (i) in aggregate do not exceed 1m² in any such wall, (provided that adjoining or contiguous windows at the junction of two walls forming an internal angle of 90 degrees or less shall be aggregated); or (ii) are glazed in an obscure material and are not able to be opened; or (iii) have a sill height not less than 1.6m above floor level.
- *mixed use development* (A1 p7): buildings that contain commercial and other non-residential uses in conjunction with residential dwellings in a multiple dwelling configuration.

### `C2.1.11` Noise sources at least 1 m from habitable room walls and 3 m from bedroom windows (R30-R60)

Facts: `dwellings.min.noise_source_to_habitable_wall_m`, `dwellings.min.noise_source_to_bedroom_window_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p17.

> Potential noise sources such as garage doors, service areas, active communal open space, communal waste storage areas and non-residential components of a mixed use development are not located within; i. 1m to the external wall of habitable rooms; and ii. 3m of a window to a bedroom.

Test: (dwellings.min.noise_source_to_habitable_wall_m >= 1.0 m AND dwellings.min.noise_source_to_bedroom_window_m >= 3.0 m).
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *communal open space* (A1 p4): outdoor areas within the lot and either at ground level or on structure that is accessible to and shared by occupants of the dwellings for communal recreational use. It does not include driveways or car parking areas.
