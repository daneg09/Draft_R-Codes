## Element objectives `O3.2`: Orientation Element Objectives

O 3.2.1 Building layouts respond to the streetscape, topography and site attributes while optimising solar and daylight access within the development. O 3.2.2 Building form and orientation minimises overshadowing of the habitable rooms, open space and solar collectors of neighbouring properties during mid-winter.

Source p28.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `building.faces_public_realm`: Buildings on the street or public realm frontage are oriented to face it (A3.2.1)? (yes/no)
- `public_domain.entry_from_street`: A3.7.x? (yes/no)
- `site.has_public_realm_frontage`: The site has a street or public realm frontage (A3.2.1 applies; A3.2.2 where false)? (yes/no)
- `overshadow.adjoining_pct`: What is the adjoining? (%; shadow cast on the adjoining property as % of its site area)
- `site.climate_zone`: What is the climate zone? (NCC climate zone (4, 5, 6 trigger overshadowing))
- `adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count`: What is the north shared boundary? (number of lots sharing the northern boundary (C3.9.4))
- `overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary`: C3.9.4? (yes/no)
- `adjoining.solar_collectors_r40_or_lower`: An adjoining site coded R40 or less has existing solar collectors (A3.2.4 trigger)? (yes/no)
- `overshadow.solar_collectors_affected`: A3.2.4? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `A3.2.1` Buildings on street or public realm frontages face the public realm with direct access from the street

Facts: `building.faces_public_realm`, `public_domain.entry_from_street`, `site.has_public_realm_frontage`.

Kind conditional; column A; coding R80+,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3,R-PA,LR,MR,HR; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p28.

> Buildings on street or public realm frontages are oriented to face the public realm and incorporate direct access from the street.

Test: (building.faces_public_realm = yes AND public_domain.entry_from_street = yes).
Applies only when: site.has_public_realm_frontage = yes.
Not met: assess against `O3.2`.

### `A3.2.2` Buildings without street or public realm frontage are oriented to maximise northern solar access to living areas

Facts: `site.has_public_realm_frontage`.

Kind judgment; column A; coding R80+,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3,R-PA,LR,MR,HR; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p28.

> Buildings that do not have frontages to streets or public realm are oriented to maximise northern solar access to living areas.

Test: judgment: no encoded test; assess against the text.
Applies only when: site.has_public_realm_frontage = no.
Not met: assess against `O3.2`.

### `A3.2.3` Overshadowing of an adjoining property at midday 21 June, climate zones 4 to 6 (25% / 35% / 50% / nil by adjoining coding)

Facts: `overshadow.adjoining_pct`, `site.climate_zone`.

Kind measurable; column A; coding R80+,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3,R-PA,LR,MR,HR; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p28.

> Development in climate zones 4, 5 and 6 shall be designed such that the shadow cast at midday on 21st June onto any adjoining property does not exceed: adjoining properties coded R25 and lower - 25% of the site area (1); adjoining properties coded R30 - R40 - 35% of the site area (1); adjoining properties coded R50 - R60 - 50% of the site area (1); adjoining properties coded R80 or higher - Nil requirements. (1) Where a development site shares its southern boundary with a lot, and that lot is bound to the north by other lot(s), the limit of shading at A 3.2.3 shall be reduced proportionally to the percentage of the affected properties northern boundary that abuts the development site.

Test: overshadow.adjoining_pct <= Table 3.2-overshadow by coding.
- Table 3.2-overshadow (A3.2.3 Maximum overshadowing of an adjoining property (percent of its site area)): R80+: no provision (R80 or higher: nil requirements); LR1/MR1/MR2/HR1/HR2/HR3/R-PA/R-AC0/R-AC1/R-AC2/R-AC3/R-AC4/LR/MR/HR: no provision (named higher-density codes read as 'R80 or higher': nil requirements).
Applies only when: (site.climate_zone >= 4 AND site.climate_zone <= 6).
Not met: assess against `O3.2`.
Defined terms:
- *adjoining property* (A1 p4): any lot: (i) on which any dwelling for which provision is made in the R-Codes may be constructed under the scheme; and (ii) which shares a boundary or portion of a boundary with a lot on which there is a proposed residential development site or is separated from that lot by a right-of-way, vehicle access way, pedestrian access way, access leg of a battleaxe lot or the equivalent not more than 6m in width.
- *site area* (A1 p9): the area of land required for the construction of a dwelling to satisfy the requirements of the R-Codes.

### `A3.2.3-r1` Proportional overshadowing limit where the affected lot's northern boundary is shared with other lots (A3.2.3 footnote 1) ⚑

Facts: `adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count`, `overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary`, `site.climate_zone`.

Kind conditional; column A; coding R80+,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3,R-PA,LR,MR,HR; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p28.

> Where a development site shares its southern boundary with a lot, and that lot is bound to the north by other lot(s), the limit of shading at A 3.2.3 shall be reduced proportionally to the percentage of the affected properties northern boundary that abuts the development site.

Test: overshadow.proportional_to_shared_boundary = yes.
Applies only when: (site.climate_zone >= 4 AND site.climate_zone <= 6); adjoining.north_shared_boundary_count >= 2.
Not met: assess against `O3.2`.
Defined terms:
- *lot boundary* (A1 p7): the boundary between a lot and any other parcel of land, excluding a street boundary.
⚑ Verification note: p28 footnote 1 says the limit is 'reduced proportionally' but gives no method or figure for the proportioning (Vol 2 Figure A7.2 deleted).

### `A3.2.4` Existing solar collectors on adjoining sites coded R40 or less keep 4 hours of sun on 21 June

Facts: `adjoining.solar_collectors_r40_or_lower`, `overshadow.solar_collectors_affected`.

Kind conditional; column A; coding R80+,LR1,MR1,MR2,HR1,HR2,HR3,R-PA,LR,MR,HR; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p28.

> Where adjoining sites are coded R40 or less, buildings are oriented to maintain 4 hours per day solar access on 21 June for existing solar collectors on neighbouring sites.

Test: overshadow.solar_collectors_affected = no.
Applies only when: adjoining.solar_collectors_r40_or_lower = yes.
Not met: assess against `O3.2`.

## Design guidance `DG3.2`: Orientation Design Guidance

DG 3.2.1 The street frontage of a development plays a role in defining the form and character of the street and should be sited and oriented accordingly. Development also contributes to the activation and surveillance of adjacent streets and should provide passive surveillance of the street and incorporate clear entries from the street to the extent possible. DG 3.2.2 Within the site, explore design solutions that configure the building plan to optimise solar access within the development, by maximising the number of dwellings and open space areas that have a northerly orientation. On sites that do not have good northern access, solar access to individual spaces may be improved through building articulation and use of courtyards. DG 3.2.3 The impact of development on solar access to properties located to the south must be considered. Measures such as increasing the setback of upper levels, breaking up building mass and orienting the development at right angles to the adjoining boundary may assist in improving solar access for neighbouring sites. In considering overshadowing of neighbouring properties priority should be given to achieving acceptable solar access to habitable rooms, outdoor living spaces and solar collectors.

Source p29.
