## Merit assessment `M2.9`: Small dwellings merit assessment criteria

M2.9.1 The design of small dwellings ensures adequate indoor and outdoor amenity for occupants. M2.9.2 Alternative and affordable housing options are provided for small households where it can be demonstrated that the development: i. is located in an area that has good access to public transport, public open space, local retailing, and other community infrastructure; ii. responds to a demand for small dwellings in the locality which may be recognised in the local planning framework; iii. provides an adequate mix of dwelling sizes; and iv. is consistent with the existing and/or future intended streetscape and local character.

Source p24.

## Provisions

### `C2.9.1` Small dwelling subject to the site area concession: maximum internal floor area 70m2

Facts: `proposal.small_dwelling`, `proposal.internal_floor_area_m2`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p24.

> Small Dwellings – Site area concession applies – Refer 4.0 Land, C4.1.5, C4.1.6 and C4.1.7. C2.9.1 Small dwellings subject to the site area concession of C4.1.5, C4.1.6 or C4.1.7 shall comply with the following: i. a maximum internal dwelling floor area of 70m2; ii. parking provided in accordance with Table 2.3a; and iii. all other provisions of the R-Codes.

Test: proposal.internal_floor_area_m2 <= 70.0 m².
Applies only when: proposal.small_dwelling = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.9`.
Defined terms:
- *small dwelling* (A1 p9): a single house or grouped dwelling with an internal floor area no greater that 70m²; or a multiple dwelling with an internal floor area no greater than 60m².
- *internal floor area* (A1 p6): in relation to a room or dwelling, the internal area measured within the finished surfaces of the walls, and includes the area occupied by any cupboard or other built-in furniture, fixture or fitting but excludes any garages.
