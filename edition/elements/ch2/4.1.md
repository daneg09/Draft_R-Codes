## Merit assessment `M4.1`: Site area merit assessment criteria

M4.1.1 Development of the type and density indicated by the density code designated in the scheme. M4.1.2 The WAPC, in consultation with the local government, may approve the creation of a green title lot or survey-strata lot (excluding battleaxe lots) with a minimum and/or average site area up to 5 per cent less than that specified in Table 2B where it will: i. not create more than one lot with a reduced minimum lots size; ii. facilitate the protection of an environmental or heritage feature; iii. facilitate the retention of a significant element that contributes toward an existing streetscape worthy of retention; iv. facilitate the development of lots with separate and sufficient frontage to more than one public street; v. overcome a special or unusual limitation on the development of the land imposed by its size, shape or other feature; vi. allow land to be developed with housing of the same type and form as land in the vicinity and which would not otherwise be able to be developed; or vii. achieve specific objectives of the local planning framework. M4.1.3 In addition to M4.1.2, the WAPC, in consultation with the local government, may approve any average lot size variation greater than five percent than that specified in Table 2B for R10 to R35 lots (base code applied for dual coded land and excludes battleaxe lots), provided that: i. the site is a corner lot with frontage to two different streets or any other lot with frontage to more than one dedicated street (excluding a primary (red), other regional (blue) or any other major road, including state and federal highways, with access restrictions); ii. all proposed lots comply with the minimum lot size and frontage provision specified in Table 2B; iii. the width and frontage of the new lots will enable dwellings to have their primary frontage to the different streets; iv. crossovers and driveways to proposed lots are provided in accordance with Australian Standard (AS) 2890 and C3.7 Access; and v. any corner truncation, pedestrian access way, vehicle right of way or laneway widening is excluded from the calculation of the minimum lot size. M4.1.4 Potential change: New provision to allow for greater subdivision potential on corner lots coded R10-R17.5. Refer to Information Sheet 5: Lot size and density. M4.1.5 Potential change: New provision to allow minimum site area to be reduced up to 10 per cent where the existing dwelling is retained. Refer to Information Sheet 5: Lot size and density.

Source p49.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `site.area_m2`: What is the area? (m²; site area)
- `site.lot_approved_by_wapc`: The lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot was approved by the WAPC at its current area (C4.1.4(i))? (yes/no)
- `site.existing_lot_with_legal_access`: Existing lot, survey-strata lot or strata lot with permanent legal access to a public road (C4.1.4(ii))? (yes/no)
- `site.r_code`: What is the r code? (code; R-coding of the lot)
- `proposal.small_dwelling`: Dwelling is a small dwelling (C2.9.1)? (yes/no)
- `proposal.universal_design_level`: What is the universal design level? (none / silver / gold; A4 or Livable Housing Australia level)
- `subdivision.lot_count`: What is the lot?
- `subdivision.reduced_lots_count`: What is the reduced lots? (lots below the minimum)
- `subdivision.proposed`: The application creates lots? (yes/no)
- `subdivision.avg_site_area_m2`: What is the avg site area? (m²)
- `subdivision.min_site_area_m2`: What is the min site area? (m²; smallest proposed site area)
- `site.battleaxe`: Lot is a battleaxe lot? (yes/no)
- `site.frontage_m`: What is the frontage? (m; frontage measured at the primary street setback line (A1 definition))
- `subdivision.min_frontage_m`: What is the min frontage? (m)
- `subdivision.battleaxe_lot`: Battleaxe lot? (yes/no)
- `subdivision.min_battleaxe_site_area_m2`: What is the min battleaxe site area? (m²; smallest proposed rear battleaxe lot area, calculated per C4.1.3(ii)-(iv) (Table 2B))

Defined terms:
- *site area* (A1 p9): the area of land required for the construction of a dwelling to satisfy the requirements of the R-Codes.
- *green title lot* (A1 p6): a lot owned in fee simple issued with a certificate of title under the Transfer of Land Act 1893, other than a strata lot or a survey-strata lot.
- *survey-strata lot* (A1 p10): refer to definition for lot in a survey-strata scheme.
- *battleaxe lot* (A1 p4): a single house lot that has a frontage for purposes of servicing and access to a public road only through a strip of connecting land containing a pedestrian access leg and/ or vehicular access way that is part of the lot. The term excludes a site that has vehicle access from a private or communal street or right-of-way connected to a public road.
- *frontage* (A1 p5): the width of a lot at the primary street setback line, provided that in the case of battleaxe or other irregularly shaped lots, it shall be as determined by the decision-maker.
- *small dwelling* (A1 p9): a single house or grouped dwelling with an internal floor area no greater that 70m²; or a multiple dwelling with an internal floor area no greater than 60m².
