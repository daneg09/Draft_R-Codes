## Merit assessment `M1.3`: Communal open space merit assessment criteria

M1.3.1 Communal open space provides: i. good quality landscaping, trees and deep soil areas; ii. safe, accessible and high amenity spaces for social interaction; iii. adequate space for its intended use and function, proportionate to the number of dwellings and number of occupants the dwellings are designed to accommodate; and iv. adequate measures to mitigate against adverse amenity impacts including visual, noise and odour.

Source p12.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `landscape.cos.area_m2`: What is the cos area? (m²; communal open space area (Table 1.3a))
- `landscape.cos.in_common_property`: COS is on common property (C1.3.1(i))? (yes/no)
- `landscape.cos.behind_street_setback`: COS is behind the street setback (C1.3.1(i))? (yes/no)
- `landscape.cos.universally_accessible`: C1.3.1(ii)? (yes/no)
- `proposal.mixed_use`: Mixed use? (yes/no)
- `landscape.cos.exclusive_to_residential`: COS is for the exclusive use of residents; mixed use only (C1.3.1(iii))? (yes/no)
- `landscape.cos.hard_landscape_area_m2`: What is the cos hard landscape area? (m²; Table 1.3a accessible/hard landscape area (2 m2 per dwelling up to 100 m2), included in the overall COS (C1.3.1-r1))
- `landscape.cos.min_dim_m`: What is the cos min dim? (m)
- `landscape.cos.max_roofed_pct`: What is the cos max roofed? (%; highest share of any one COS area under a covered roof (C1.3.1-r3, 25% of each area))
- `landscape.cos.separated_from_noise_odour`: C1.3.2? (yes/no)
- `landscape.cos.oriented_to_limit_impacts`: C1.3.3? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C1.3.1` Communal open space: 6 m2 per dwelling up to 300 m2 (Table 1.3a)

Facts: `proposal.dwelling_count`, `landscape.cos.area_m2`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p12 Table 1.3a.

> C1.3.1 Communal open space is provided for apartment development in accordance with Table 1.3a and the following: i. located in common property and behind the primary street setback line; ii. to be universally accessible to all occupants of the development; and iii. exclusive to the residential component of mixed-use development. Table 1.3a Provision of communal open space. Up to 10 dwellings: No provision. More than 10 dwellings: minimum communal open space provision 6m2 per dwelling up to maximum 300m2. Communal open spaces can be co-located with deep soil areas, soft landscaping area and/or co-indoor communal spaces.

Test: landscape.cos.area_m2 >= min(6 * proposal.dwelling_count, 300).
Applies only when: proposal.dwelling_count > 10.
Not met: assess against `M1.3`.
Defined terms:
- *communal open space* (A1 p4): outdoor areas within the lot and either at ground level or on structure that is accessible to and shared by occupants of the dwellings for communal recreational use. It does not include driveways or car parking areas.

### `C1.3.3` Communal open space designed and oriented to minimise noise, odour, lightspill and overlooking

Facts: `landscape.cos.area_m2`, `landscape.cos.oriented_to_limit_impacts`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p12.

> C1.3.3 Communal open space is designed and oriented to minimise the impacts of noise, odour, lightspill and overlooking on the habitable rooms and private open spaces within the site and of adjoining properties.

Test: landscape.cos.oriented_to_limit_impacts = yes.
Applies only when: landscape.cos.area_m2 > 0 m².
Not met: assess against `M1.3`.
Defined terms:
- *habitable room/space* (A1 p6): as defined by the NCC for a room/space used for normal domestic activities that includes: (i) a bedroom, living room, lounge room, music room, sitting room, television room, kitchen, scullery, dining room, sewing room, study, playroom, family room, sunroom, gymnasium, fully enclosed swimming pool or patio; but excludes: (i) a bathroom, laundry, water closet, pantry, walk-in wardrobe, corridor, hallway, lobby, photographic darkroom, clothes drying room, verandah and unenclosed swimming pool or patio and other spaces of a specialised nature occupied neither frequently nor for extended periods.
- *private open space* (A1 p8): means outdoor space located at ground level or on a structure that is within private ownership and provided for the recreational use of residents of the dwelling to which it abuts and excludes car parking spaces and access ways. It includes primary garden areas, outdoor living areas, balconies, courtyards and terraces.
