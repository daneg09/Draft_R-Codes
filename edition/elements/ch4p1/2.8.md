## Merit assessment `M2.8`: Ancillary dwellings merit assessment criteria

M2.8.1 The size of an ancillary dwelling allows for adequate internal amenity whilst remaining incidental to the primary dwelling. M2.8.2 Ancillary dwellings allow for diversity of housing while minimising adverse impacts on the amenity of: i. adjoining properties; ii. streetscape and local character; and iii. the primary dwelling.

Source p24.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `proposal.ancillary_dwelling`: Proposal includes an ancillary dwelling? (yes/no)
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `ancillary.dual_key`: Every ancillary dwelling is a dual key dwelling (Table 2.8a, dual key only)? (yes/no)
- `ancillary.count`: What is the count? (ancillary dwellings on the site)
- `ancillary.internal_floor_area_m2`: What is the internal floor area? (m²)

## Provisions

### `C2.8.1` Ancillary dwelling: dual key only, number and maximum 70 m2 per Table 2.8a ⚑

Facts: `proposal.ancillary_dwelling`, `proposal.dwelling_count`, `ancillary.dual_key`, `ancillary.count`, `ancillary.internal_floor_area_m2`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling, ancillary_dwelling; source p24 Table 2.8a.

> An ancillary dwelling in accordance with Table 2.8a, provided that it: i. does not preclude the primary dwelling from meeting the maximum site cover and the minimum required private open space, soft landscaping, trees and deep soil area; and ii. complies with the following design elements as relevant: 2.2 Solar access and natural ventilation, 2.3 Parking, 2.5 Utilities, 3.1 Site cover, 3.2 Building height, 3.3 Street setbacks, 3.4 Lot boundary setbacks, 3.5 Site works and retaining walls, 3.6 Streetscape, 3.7 Access, 3.8 Solar access for adjoining sites, 3.9 Visual privacy. Table 2.8a Ancillary dwelling provisions (number of apartments; ancillary dwelling type; maximum ancillary dwellings; maximum internal floor area): 1-19 dwellings: dual key dwelling only; 1 per development; 70m2. 20 or more dwellings: dual key dwelling only; 2 per development and 1 per additional 10 dwellings¹; 70m2. ¹Rounded down to the nearest 10 dwellings. Note: The above provisions relate to the ancillary dwelling portion of the development, with the exception of 3.1 Site cover, 3.4 Lot boundary setbacks and 3.8 Solar access for adjoining sites which would need to be assessed in combination with the primary dwelling. The primary dwelling would still need to comply with all relevant provisions of the R-Codes.

Test: (ancillary.dual_key = yes AND ancillary.count <= 1 if proposal.dwelling_count < 20 else 2 + round((proposal.dwelling_count - 24.5) / 10) AND ancillary.internal_floor_area_m2 <= Table 2.8a by dwelling_count).
- Table 2.8a (Table 2.8a Ancillary dwelling provisions (maximum internal floor area)): dwelling_count 1-19: 70 m2 (1-19 dwellings: dual key dwelling only; maximum 1 per development); dwelling_count >= 20: 70 m2 (20 or more dwellings: dual key dwelling only; maximum 2 per development and 1 per additional 10 dwellings (rounded down to the nearest 10)).
Applies only when: proposal.ancillary_dwelling = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.8`.
Defined terms:
- *ancillary dwelling* (A1 p4): self-contained dwelling on the same site as a dwelling which may be attached to, integrated with or detached from the dwelling.
- *internal floor area* (A1 p6): in relation to a room or dwelling, the internal area measured within the finished surfaces of the walls, and includes the area occupied by any cupboard or other built-in furniture, fixture or fitting but excludes any garages.
⚑ Verification note: Table 2.8a (p24) heads the first column 'Number of apartments' but labels rows 'dwellings'; whether the count includes the ancillary dwellings is not stated. Values (dual key only, 1 / 2 + 1 per additional 10 rounded down, 70 m2) confirmed.
