## Merit assessment `M2.7`: Universal design merit assessment criteria

M2.7.1 Development provides accessible housing or adaptable dwellings that are proportionate in number to the size of the development and intended occupancy. M2.7.2 Accessible housing and adaptable dwellings provide housing options for people with disabilities and/or limited or reduced mobility, facilitate ageing in place, and are designed in accordance with the universal design requirements: i. of the Livable Housing Design Guidelines; ii. of the Australian Building Codes Board Livable Housing Design Standard (2022); iii. of AS4299; and/or iv. to suit the specific needs of the occupant.

Source p23.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `proposal.dwellings_universal_design_pct`: What is the dwellings universal design? (%; % of dwellings meeting silver level (C2.7.1))
- `proposal.seeks_universal_design_site_area_variation`: The application seeks the gold-level universal design site area variation under C4.1.6 or C4.1.7 (C2.7.1)? (yes/no)
- `proposal.universal_design_level`: What is the universal design level? (none / silver / gold; A4 or Livable Housing Australia level)
- `proposal.internal_floor_area_m2`: What is the internal floor area? (m²)

## Provisions

### `C2.7.1` Ten or more apartments: at least 20% of dwellings at silver level universal design

Facts: `proposal.dwelling_count`, `proposal.dwellings_universal_design_pct`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p23.

> Where 10 or more apartments are proposed, a minimum 20 per cent¹ of all dwellings are: i. designed and constructed to a minimum silver level universal design in accordance with A4 Universal design requirements, or ii. certified Livable Housing Australia to a minimum silver level of performance. Note: No universal design provisions apply for apartments development with less than 10 dwellings. All other provisions of the R-Codes still apply. ¹Where calculations result in a fraction of a dwelling, the provision is to be rounded up to the nearest whole number.

Test: proposal.dwellings_universal_design_pct >= 20%.
Applies only when: proposal.dwelling_count >= 10.
Not met: assess against `M2.7`.
Defined terms:
- *universal design* (A1 p10): the design of products and environments that are inherently accessible to all, including older people and people with disability.

### `C2.7.2` Gold-level universal design dwelling seeking the site area variation: gold level and maximum 90 m2

Facts: `proposal.seeks_universal_design_site_area_variation`, `proposal.universal_design_level`, `proposal.internal_floor_area_m2`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p23.

> Accessible dwellings that seek to apply the gold level universal design site area variation as part of 4.0 - Land, C4.1.5 or C4.1.6 shall; i. be designed and constructed in accordance with the gold level universal design requirements of A4 Universal design requirements, or are certified Livable Housing Australia to a minimum gold level of performance; and ii. have a maximum internal floor area of 90m2. Note: All other provisions of the R-Codes still apply.

Test: (proposal.universal_design_level == gold AND proposal.internal_floor_area_m2 <= 90.0 m²).
Applies only when: proposal.seeks_universal_design_site_area_variation = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.7`.
Defined terms:
- *internal floor area* (A1 p6): in relation to a room or dwelling, the internal area measured within the finished surfaces of the walls, and includes the area occupied by any cupboard or other built-in furniture, fixture or fitting but excludes any garages.
