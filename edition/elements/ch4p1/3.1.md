## Merit assessment `M3.1`: Site cover merit assessment criteria

M3.1.1 The site cover of the development is suitable for its context to: i. achieve appropriate building bulk on the site, consistent with the intent of the applicable density code and/or as outlined in the local planning framework; ii. ensure sufficient outdoor space for landscaping including trees and deep soil areas; iii. ensure adequate solar access and natural ventilation into the dwelling; iv. provide opportunities for residents to use space external to the dwelling for outdoor pursuits and access within and around the site; v. provide space for utilities and essential facilities; and vi. be compatible with the existing and/or desired streetscape and local character.

Source p27.

## Provisions

### `C3.1.1` Maximum site cover

Facts: `building.roofed_area_m2`, `site.area_m2`, `site.r_code`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p27 Table 3.1a.

> C3.1.1 Development on each site does not exceed the maximum site cover percentages of Table 3.1a. Table 3.1a Maximum site cover: R10 45%; R12.5 50%; R15-R25 55%. Site cover does not apply to R30 and above.

Test: building.roofed_area_m2 as % of site.area_m2 <= Table 3.1a by coding.
- Table 3.1a (Table 3.1a Maximum site cover): R10: 45 %; R12.5: 50 %; R15-R25: 55 %; R30-R60: no provision (Site cover does not apply to R30 and above (Table 3.1a note; register C4P1-068)).
Not met: assess against `M3.1`.
Defined terms:
- *site cover* (A1 p9): the area occupied by any building, including upper storeys or balconies projecting beyond the ground floor building alignment, or other structures roofed with impervious material, but excludes: (i) uncovered driveways and parking spaces; (ii) eaves and pergolas; and (iii) a basement that is constructed wholly underground.
