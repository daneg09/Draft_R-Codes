## Merit assessment `M2.3`: Parking merit assessment criteria

M2.3.1 Siting and the extent of parking does not dominate the development or streetscape and responds to the context and local character by: i. supporting the efficient use of the site; and ii. minimising the extent of paving for parking and vehicle access. M2.3.2 Adequate parking is provided for various modes of transport, including bicycles, motorcycles, scooters and cars, that has regard to the following considerations: i. the proximity of the proposed development to public transport, activity centres, areas of amenity and other facilities; ii. the type, size and number of dwellings; and iii. the availability of on-street and other off-street parking. M2.3.3 Parking spaces are designed for flexibility and adaptability having regard for: i. the needs of occupants, including consideration of universally accessible parking spaces; and ii. the ability to use the space for alternative purposes.

Source p18.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `parking.spaces`: What is the spaces? (on-site car parking spaces for the dwelling)
- `site.location`: What is the location? (A / B; parking Location A or B (Table 2.3a note))
- `proposal.bedrooms`: What is the bedrooms? (0 for studio)
- `proposal.ancillary_dwelling`: Proposal includes an ancillary dwelling? (yes/no)
- `parking.ancillary_spaces`: What is the ancillary spaces? (spaces for the ancillary dwelling)
- `parking.garage_carport_spaces`: What is the garage carport spaces? (spaces within garages and carports)
- `parking.ancillary_garage_carport_spaces`: What is the ancillary garage carport spaces? (garage and carport spaces serving the ancillary dwelling (Tables 2.3a, 2.3b ancillary maximum))
- `parking.as2890_compliant`: Spaces and manoeuvring per AS2890.1? (yes/no)
