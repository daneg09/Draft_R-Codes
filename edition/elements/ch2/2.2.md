## Merit assessment `M2.2`: Solar access and natural ventilation merit assessment criteria

M2.2.1 In climate zones 4, 5 and 6 the development is sited, oriented and designed to optimise winter solar gain whilst limiting summer sunlight into: i. the primary living space and habitable rooms; and ii. private open spaces, including the primary garden area; while balancing site constraints, outlook and views of significance. M2.2.2 Windows to habitable rooms are designed and positioned to optimize daylight, natural ventilation and outlook, while maintaining a reasonable level of visual privacy. M2.2.3 Dwellings optimize natural ventilation to habitable rooms (and bathrooms where possible) that is responsive to site and local climatic conditions.

Source p16.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `building.courtyard_daylight_only`: A courtyard is the sole daylight source to a habitable room (C2.2.1)? (yes/no)
- `building.courtyard_open_to_sky`: The courtyard is uncovered and open to the sky, minor projections excluded (C2.2.1)? (yes/no)
- `building.courtyard_area_m2`: What is the courtyard area? (m²)
- `site.climate_zone`: What is the climate zone? (NCC climate zone (4, 5, 6 trigger overshadowing))
- `building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation`: What is the primary living major opening orientation? (enum; NW, N, NE, E, other (C2.2.2))
- `building.primary_living_uncovered_min_dim_m`: What is the primary living uncovered min dim? (m)
- `building.primary_living_uncovered_area_m2`: What is the primary living uncovered area? (m²; uncovered area adjoining that opening)
- `site.structure_plan_or_ldp_approved_before_2024_04_10`: Lot is within a structure plan and/or local development plan approved before 10 April 2024 (C2.2.2 transitional footnote, until 10 April 2030)? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C2.2.1` Courtyard that is the only daylight source to a habitable room is uncovered and at least 4m2

Facts: `building.courtyard_daylight_only`, `building.courtyard_open_to_sky`, `building.courtyard_area_m2`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R50+,R100-SL; applies to single_house; source p16.

> Windows and openings (applies to R50-R80; R100-SL; LR, MR, and HR only). C2.2.1 Where a courtyard is the only source of daylight to a habitable room, the courtyard must be uncovered and open to the sky(1) with a: i. minimum area of 4m2 (refer Figure 2.2a). (1) Excludes minor projections

Test: (building.courtyard_open_to_sky = yes AND building.courtyard_area_m2 >= 4.0 m²).
Applies only when: building.courtyard_daylight_only = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.2`.
Defined terms:
- *habitable room/space* (A1 p6): as defined by the NCC for a room/space used for normal domestic activities that includes: (i) a bedroom, living room, lounge room, music room, sitting room, television room, kitchen, scullery, dining room, sewing room, study, playroom, family room, sunroom, gymnasium, fully enclosed swimming pool or patio; but excludes: (i) a bathroom, laundry, water closet, pantry, walk-in wardrobe, corridor, hallway, lobby, photographic darkroom, clothes drying room, verandah and unenclosed swimming pool or patio and other spaces of a specialised nature occupied neither frequently nor for extended periods.
- *major opening* (A1 p7): a window, door or other opening in the exterior wall of a habitable room that provides external means of light or view for that room or space, but does not include an opening or openings that: (i) in aggregate do not exceed 1m² in any such wall, (provided that adjoining or contiguous windows at the junction of two walls forming an internal angle of 90 degrees or less shall be aggregated); or (ii) are glazed in an obscure material and are not able to be opened; or (iii) have a sill height not less than 1.6m above floor level.

### `C2.2.2` Major opening to the primary living space oriented north-west to east with a 3 m x 3 m uncovered area

Facts: `site.climate_zone`, `building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation`, `building.primary_living_uncovered_min_dim_m`, `building.primary_living_uncovered_area_m2`, `site.structure_plan_or_ldp_approved_before_2024_04_10`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R50+,R100-SL; applies to single_house; source p17.

> Orientation of major openings (applies to R50-R80; R100-SL; LR, MR, and HR only). Note: No orientation provisions apply to primary living areas located in climate zones 1 and 3. C2.2.2 For single houses(1) in climate zones 4,5 and 6, a major opening to the primary living space is oriented between north-west and east in accordance with Figure 2.2b, with an adjoining uncovered open area with: i. a minimum dimension 3m x 3m(2) in accordance with Figure 2.2c; and ii. the exception of shading devices up to 2m depth. (1) Until 10 April 2030, this provision does not apply to single houses located in areas designated with a residential density code of R50 or higher within a structure plan and/or local development plan that was approved prior to 10 April 2024. (2) The centre line of the minimum 3m x 3m area must be contained within the glazed area of the major opening (Figure 2.2d).

Test: ((building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation == NW OR building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation == N OR building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation == NE OR building.primary_living_major_opening_orientation == E) AND building.primary_living_uncovered_min_dim_m >= 3.0 m AND building.primary_living_uncovered_area_m2 >= 9.0 m²).
Applies only when: site.climate_zone >= 4; site.climate_zone <= 6.
Exception 1: transitional: R50 or higher site within a structure plan or LDP approved before 10 April 2024 (until 10 April 2030). When site.structure_plan_or_ldp_approved_before_2024_04_10 = yes: the provision does not apply. (C2.2.2 footnote 1, p17)
Not met: assess against `M2.2`.
Defined terms:
- *primary living space* (A1 p8): the identified room(s) within a dwelling that is the focus of life and activity and usually the largest room. This area is connected with private open space and may include any of the following room types: living room, lounge room, family room, or an integrated open plan living area that has one of these room types together with a kitchen or dining area.
- *climate zone* (A1 p4): as defined by the NCC. Western Australia is divided into five climate zones based on humidity and temperature, ranging from temperate in the southwest to hot/arid in the interior to hot/humid in the north. Refer Climate Zones Map.
