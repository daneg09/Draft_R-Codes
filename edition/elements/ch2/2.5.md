## Merit assessment `M2.5`: Utilities merit assessment criteria

M2.5.1 The site is serviced with utilities that that are fit for purpose and meet current performance and access requirements of service providers, addressing access, maintenance and safety considerations. M2.5.2 Utilities balance operational requirements with the need to minimise: i. visual, noise, heat and air quality impacts on habitable rooms and private open space both on the development site and adjoining properties; and ii. the visual impact on the streetscape.

Source p20.

## Provisions

### `C2.5.1` Service utilities accessible, with clear sightlines, integrated or screened from the street

Facts: `services.service_utilities_accessible_screened`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p20.

> C2.5.1 Service utilities are designed and located such that they: i. are accessible and can be safely maintained; ii. maintain clear sightlines for vehicle access; and iii. integrated into the design of the development and/or screened from view of the street. Note: Where required by the NCC, fire service infrastructure is located to be visible, and with unobstructed access for its required use during an emergency.

Test: services.service_utilities_accessible_screened = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.5`.
Defined terms:
- *service utilities* (A1 p9): supply and reticulation of essential services including, but not limited to power, water, gas, wastewater, fire services, letterboxes and telecommunications.
- *street* (A1 p10): any public road, communal street, private street, laneway, right-of-way or other shared access way that provides the principal frontage to a dwelling but does not include an access leg to a single battleaxe lot.

### `C2.5.2` Functional utilities behind the primary street setback and not visible from the primary street

Facts: `services.functional_utilities_behind_setback`, `services.functional_utilities_not_visible_from_street`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p20.

> C2.5.2 Functional utilities (with the exception of solar collectors and electric vehicle charging): i. are located behind the primary street setback and not visible from the primary street; ii. are designed to integrate with the development; and iii. are located and/or screened so that they are not visually obtrusive and minimise the impact of noise sources to habitable rooms and private open space both on the development site and adjoining properties.

Test: (services.functional_utilities_behind_setback = yes AND services.functional_utilities_not_visible_from_street = yes).
Not met: assess against `M2.5`.
Defined terms:
- *functional utilities* (A1 p5): functional utilities associated with the dwelling to achieve efficient, comfortable and environmentally sustainable operating outcomes including, but not limited to air-conditioning, plant, clothes drying, hot water systems, antennas and satellite dishes, solar collectors, inverters and batteries, electric vehicle charging and rainwater tanks.
- *primary street* (A1 p8): unless otherwise designated by the local government, the sole or principal public road that provides access to the major entry (front door) to the dwelling or building.
