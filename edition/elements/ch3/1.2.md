## Merit assessment `M1.2`: Trees and landscaping merit assessment criteria

M1.2.1 Site planning allows for: i. site responsive and sustainable landscape design; and ii. the retention of existing trees on the subject site and adjoining properties. M1.2.2 Provision of trees and high quality landscaping: i. enhances the built form, streetscape and pedestrian amenity, as viewed from the street; ii. provides shade and amenity for communal streets and parking areas; and iii. contributes to the visual appeal, comfort and amenity of the development, in particular private open space and communal open space and outlook from habitable rooms. M1.2.3 Development provides sufficient deep soil area to sustain healthy tree and plant growth, providing for an increase in urban tree canopy over time, and assist in managing the quantity and quality of stormwater.

Source p11.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `landscape.soft_area_m2`: What is the soft area? (m²; soft landscape area per the A1 definition)
- `site.area_m2`: What is the area? (m²; site area)
- `site.significant_tree_retained`: A significant existing tree is retained (C1.2.4)? (yes/no)
- `landscape.soft_min_dim_m`: What is the soft min dim? (m)
- `landscape.street_setback_soft_area_m2`: What is the street setback soft area? (m²; soft landscape within it)
- `landscape.street_setback_area_m2`: What is the street setback area? (m²; area of the primary street setback area)
- `site.has_communal_street`: Has communal street? (yes/no)
- `site.communal_street_landscaped_and_lit`: C1.2.3? (yes/no)
- `landscape.trees_in_street_setback`: What is the trees in street setback? (small trees in the primary street setback area)
- `site.frontage_m`: What is the frontage? (m; frontage measured at the primary street setback line (A1 definition))
- `building.primary_street_setback_m`: What is the primary street setback? (m; minimum setback of the dwelling to the primary street boundary)
- `landscape.trees_elsewhere`: What is the trees elsewhere? (small trees elsewhere on the lot)
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `landscape.tree_deep_soil_min_m2`: What is the tree deep soil min? (m²; smallest deep soil area provided to any required tree)
- `landscape.tree_deep_soil_min_dim_m`: What is the tree deep soil min dim? (m)
- `parking.uncovered_at_grade_spaces`: What is the uncovered at grade spaces? (number of uncovered at-grade car parking spaces on the site (C1.2.5))
- `landscape.parking_shade_trees`: What is the parking shade trees? (small shade trees planted to serve the uncovered at-grade parking, additional to the C1.2.4 trees (C1.2.5))
- `site.retained_tree_root_zone_provided`: Nominal root zone per AS4970 (C1.2.5)? (yes/no)
- `landscape.landscaping_plan_provided`: C1.2.8? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C1.2.1` Soft landscaping minimum 15% of the site ⚑

Facts: `landscape.soft_area_m2`, `site.area_m2`, `site.significant_tree_retained`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R2-R80,R100,R160,LR,MR,HR; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p11.

> Landscaping. C1.2.1 Development to provide a minimum 15% soft landscaping per site with a minimum dimension of 1m (refer Figure 1.2a). C1.2.6 The soft landscaping percentage of C1.2.1 may be reduced to 10% where a significant existing tree is retained on site. Note: a retained tree replaces the tree planting provision in C1.2.4 on a like-for-like basis.

Test: landscape.soft_area_m2 as % of site.area_m2 >= 15%.
Exception 1: C1.2.6 significant existing tree retained on site: soft landscaping reduced to 10%. When site.significant_tree_retained = yes: sets value = 10. (C1.2.6 (register ch3-014))
Not met: assess against `M1.2`.
Defined terms:
- *soft landscape* (A1 p9): any landscaped area with a minimum soil depth of 300mm that contains in-ground planting and excludes removable planter boxes/pots and permeable paving areas. Turf is included.
- *site area* (A1 p9): the area of land required for the construction of a dwelling to satisfy the requirements of the R-Codes.
- *significant existing tree* (A1 p9): an existing tree that meets the following criteria: (i) healthy specimens with ongoing viability; and (ii) species is not included on a State or local area weed register; and (iii) height of at least 4m; and/or (iv) trunk diameter of at least 160mm, measured 1m from the ground; and/or (v) average canopy diameter of at least 4m; or (vi) as specified with the local planning framework.
⚑ Verification note: Values (15%, 1m; 10% with a retained significant tree, C1.2.6 on p12) check. 'Per site' does not say whether a grouped development is tested per dwelling site or across the whole development; Figure 1.2a shows several dwellings without settling it (ch3-009).
