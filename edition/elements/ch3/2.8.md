## Merit assessment `M2.8`: Ancillary dwellings merit assessment criteria

M2.8.1 The size of an ancillary dwelling allows for adequate internal amenity whilst remaining incidental to the primary dwelling. M2.8.2 Ancillary dwellings allow for diversity of housing while minimising adverse impacts on the amenity of: i. adjoining properties; ii. streetscape and local character; and iii. the primary dwelling.

Source p24.

## Provisions

### `C2.8.1` Ancillary dwelling: maximum one per site and maximum internal floor area per Table 2.8a

Facts: `proposal.ancillary_dwelling`, `ancillary.count`, `ancillary.internal_floor_area_m2`, `ancillary.on_same_site_as_small_dwelling`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling, ancillary_dwelling; source p24 Table 2.8a.

> C2.8.1 An ancillary dwelling in accordance with Table 2.8a, provided that it: i. does not preclude the primary dwelling from meeting the maximum site cover and the minimum required private open space, soft landscaping, trees and deep soil area; and ii. complies with the following design elements as relevant: 2.2 Solar access and natural ventilation; 2.3 Parking; 2.5 Utilities; 3.1 Site cover; 3.2 Building height; 3.3 Street setbacks; 3.4 Lot boundary setbacks; 3.5 Site works and retaining walls; 3.6 Streetscape; 3.7 Access; 3.9 Solar access for adjoining sites; 3.10 Visual privacy. Table 2.8a Ancillary dwelling provisions: when provided on the same site as a Small Dwelling in accordance with C2.9.1 - maximum 1 per site, maximum internal floor area 70m2; all other primary dwellings - maximum 1 per site, maximum internal floor area 90m2.

Test: (ancillary.count <= 1 AND ancillary.internal_floor_area_m2 <= Table 2.8a by on_same_site_as_small_dwelling).
- Table 2.8a (Table 2.8a Ancillary dwelling provisions (maximum internal floor area)): on_same_site_as_small_dwelling yes: 70 m2 (when provided on the same site as a small dwelling in accordance with C2.9.1; maximum 1 per site); on_same_site_as_small_dwelling no: 90 m2 (all other primary dwellings; maximum 1 per site).
Applies only when: proposal.ancillary_dwelling = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.8`.
Defined terms:
- *ancillary dwelling* (A1 p4): self-contained dwelling on the same site as a dwelling which may be attached to, integrated with or detached from the dwelling.
- *internal floor area* (A1 p6): in relation to a room or dwelling, the internal area measured within the finished surfaces of the walls, and includes the area occupied by any cupboard or other built-in furniture, fixture or fitting but excludes any garages.
- *small dwelling* (A1 p9): a single house or grouped dwelling with an internal floor area no greater that 70m²; or a multiple dwelling with an internal floor area no greater than 60m².
