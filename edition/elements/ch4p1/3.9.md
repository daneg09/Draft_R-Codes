## Merit assessment `M3.9`: Visual privacy merit assessment criteria

M3.9.1 Direct overlooking of major openings and active habitable spaces of adjacent dwellings and adjoining properties minimised through: i. building siting, layout and design; ii. design and location of major openings; iii. landscape screening of outdoor active habitable spaces; and/or iv. design and location of screening devices. M3.9.2 Adequate visual privacy achieved through appropriate interfaces between dwellings and adjoining properties including measures such as: i. offsetting the location of ground and first floor windows so that viewing is oblique rather than direct; ii. building boundary walls where appropriate; iii. setting back the upper storeys from the lot boundary; iv. providing higher or lower windows, or windows with obscure glazing; and/or v. screening (including landscaping, fencing, timber screens, external blinds, window hoods and shutters). M3.9.3 Visual privacy strategies maintain amenity of habitable rooms and active habitable space with regard to solar access, natural ventilation and external outlook both within the development and for adjoining properties.

Source p44.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `adjoining.vacant_or_unknown`: Adjoining site vacant or dwelling layout unknown (C3.10.4)? (yes/no)
- `privacy.bedroom_study_setback_m`: What is the bedroom study setback? (m; minimum cone-of-vision setback from a bedroom or study major opening)
- `privacy.cone_captures_adjoining_opening`: Cone captures a major opening or active habitable space of an existing adjoining dwelling? (yes/no)
- `privacy.sources_above_0_5m`: Any major opening or active habitable space with floor level more than 0.5 m above NGL? (yes/no)
- `privacy.other_habitable_setback_m`: What is the other habitable setback? (m; from other habitable room major openings)
- `privacy.outdoor_active_setback_m`: What is the outdoor active setback? (m; from outdoor active habitable spaces)
- `privacy.cone_forward_of_adjoining_setback_line`: C3.10.2? (yes/no)
- `privacy.screened_or_offset`: Fallback screening or 1.5 m offset (C3.10.3)? (yes/no)
- `privacy.bedroom_study_offset_m`: What is the bedroom study offset? (m; edge-to-edge offset between a bedroom/study major opening and the parallel adjoining opening (C3.10.3))
- `privacy.between_apartments_same_lot_compliant`: C3.9.4? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C3.9.1-r1` Cone of vision setback from a bedroom or study major opening: 3 m

Facts: `adjoining.vacant_or_unknown`, `privacy.bedroom_study_setback_m`, `privacy.cone_captures_adjoining_opening`, `privacy.sources_above_0_5m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p44 Table 3.9a.

> For development adjoining an existing dwelling. C3.9.1 All sources of overlooking are oriented, offset or setback in accordance with Table 3.9a so that the cone of vision (refer Figure 3.9a) does not capture major openings and/or active habitable spaces on an adjoining property (refer Figure 3.9b). Table 3.9a Cone of vision radius (type of habitable room/active habitable space with a floor level of more than 0.5m above natural ground level): Major opening from bedroom and study: 3m.

Test: privacy.bedroom_study_setback_m >= 3.0 m.
Applies only when: privacy.sources_above_0_5m = yes; adjoining.vacant_or_unknown = no.
Exception 1: cone of vision does not capture a major opening or active habitable space of the adjoining existing dwelling. When privacy.cone_captures_adjoining_opening = no: the provision does not apply.
Not met: assess against `M3.9`.
Defined terms:
- *cone of vision* (A1 p5): the limits of outlook from any given viewpoint for the purposes of assessing the extent of overlooking from that point, measured by applying a viewing cut off angle not less than 45 degrees horizontally and extending 90 degrees down to the ground.
- *major opening* (A1 p7): a window, door or other opening in the exterior wall of a habitable room that provides external means of light or view for that room or space, but does not include an opening or openings that: (i) in aggregate do not exceed 1m² in any such wall, (provided that adjoining or contiguous windows at the junction of two walls forming an internal angle of 90 degrees or less shall be aggregated); or (ii) are glazed in an obscure material and are not able to be opened; or (iii) have a sill height not less than 1.6m above floor level.
- *active habitable space* (A1 p4): any: (i) habitable room with a internal floor area greater than 10m²; and (ii) balcony, courtyard, patio, verandah or other forms of private open space.
- *adjoining property* (A1 p4): any lot: (i) on which any dwelling for which provision is made in the R-Codes may be constructed under the scheme; and (ii) which shares a boundary or portion of a boundary with a lot on which there is a proposed residential development site or is separated from that lot by a right-of-way, vehicle access way, pedestrian access way, access leg of a battleaxe lot or the equivalent not more than 6m in width.
- *natural ground level* (A1 p7): the levels on a site which precede the proposed development, excluding any site works unless approved by the decision-maker or established as part of subdivision of the land preceding development.

### `C3.9.2` Screening where the cone of vision captures an existing dwelling's opening

Facts: `adjoining.vacant_or_unknown`, `privacy.cone_captures_adjoining_opening`, `privacy.cone_forward_of_adjoining_setback_line`, `privacy.screened_or_offset`, `privacy.sources_above_0_5m`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p45.

> Notwithstanding C3.9.1, where the cone of vision captures a major opening or an active habitable space of an existing dwelling behind the greater of the street setback or street setback line on an adjoining property, the source of overlooking is designed to limit or interrupt the line of sight into the major opening or active habitable space of the adjoining property through one or more of the following: i. incorporate a permanent, fixed vertical or horizontal building element such as a planter box, fin or window hood (refer Figure 3.9c, Figure 3.9d); ii. have permanent, obscure glazing in any part of the window below 1.6m above floor level (refer Figure 3.9e); or iii. have permanent screening that is a minimum 75 per cent obscure to any part of the window or active habitable space below 1.6m above floor level (refer Figure 3.9f).

Test: privacy.screened_or_offset = yes.
Applies only when: privacy.sources_above_0_5m = yes; adjoining.vacant_or_unknown = no; privacy.cone_captures_adjoining_opening = yes.
Exception 1: cone falls forward of the adjoining property's street setback or street setback line, whichever is greater. When privacy.cone_forward_of_adjoining_setback_line = yes: the provision does not apply.
Not met: assess against `M3.9`.
Defined terms:
- *street setback* (A1 p10): the horizontal distance between the street boundary and a building, measured at right angles (90 degrees) to the street boundary.
- *street setback line* (A1 p10): the minimum distance between the street boundary and a building in accordance with Part B, Table B and Part C, Table 3.3a.
