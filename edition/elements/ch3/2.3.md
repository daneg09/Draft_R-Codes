## Merit assessment `M2.3`: Parking merit assessment criteria

M2.3.1 Siting and the extent of parking does not dominate the development or streetscape and responds to the context and local character by: i. supporting the efficient use of the site; and ii. minimising the extent of paving for parking and vehicle access. M2.3.2 Adequate parking is provided for various modes of transport, including bicycles, motorcycles, scooters and cars, that has regard to the following considerations: i. the proximity of the proposed development to public transport, activity centres, areas of amenity and other facilities; ii. the type, size and number of dwellings; and iii. the availability of on-street and other off-street parking. M2.3.3 Parking spaces are designed for flexibility and adaptability having regard for: i. the needs of occupants, including consideration of universally accessible parking spaces; and ii. the ability to use the space for alternative purposes.

Source p18.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `parking.spaces`: What is the spaces? (on-site car parking spaces for the dwelling)
- `site.location`: What is the location? (A / B; parking Location A or B (Table 2.3a note))
- `proposal.bedrooms`: What is the bedrooms? (0 for studio)
- `proposal.ancillary_dwelling`: Proposal includes an ancillary dwelling? (yes/no)
- `parking.ancillary_spaces`: What is the ancillary spaces? (spaces for the ancillary dwelling)
- `parking.garage_carport_spaces`: What is the garage carport spaces? (spaces within garages and carports)
- `parking.ancillary_garage_carport_spaces`: What is the ancillary garage carport spaces? (garage and carport spaces serving the ancillary dwelling (Tables 2.3a, 2.3b ancillary maximum))
- `parking.as2890_compliant`: Spaces and manoeuvring per AS2890.1? (yes/no)
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)
- `parking.visitor_spaces`: What is the visitor spaces? (visitor bays on common property (C2.3.3, C2.3.7))
- `parking.visitor_spaces_marked_and_accessible`: C2.3.4? (yes/no)

### `C2.3.3` Visitor parking for 5 or more grouped dwellings, Perth and Peel (Table 2.3a: 0.1 bays per dwelling Location A, 0.15 Location B)

Facts: `proposal.dwelling_count`, `site.location`, `parking.visitor_spaces`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; region perth-peel; source p18 Table 2.3a.

> Visitor parking. C2.3.3 Visitor parking for grouped dwellings is provided on site in accordance with Table 2.3a. Table 2.3a visitor car parking, minimum parking space: Location A - 0-4 dwellings no visitor parking required; 5+ dwellings 0.1 bays per dwelling for the total number of dwellings. Location B - 0-4 dwellings no visitor parking required; 5+ dwellings 0.15 bays per dwelling for the total number of dwellings. Calculations of parking ratios shall be rounded to the nearest whole number, with fractions of 0.5 or greater rounded up (for example, 1.4 bays is rounded to 1 bay and 1.5 bays is rounded to 2 bays).

Test: parking.visitor_spaces >= Table 2.3a-visitor-bays by location.
- Table 2.3a-visitor-bays (Table 2.3a visitor car parking: required bays for the total number of dwellings, by location): location A: round(0.1 * proposal.dwelling_count + 0.001) (nearest whole number, 0.5 rounds up) bays (expression over proposal.dwelling_count) (Location A: 0.1 bays per dwelling); location B: round(0.15 * proposal.dwelling_count + 0.001) (nearest whole number, 0.5 rounds up) bays (expression over proposal.dwelling_count) (Location B: 0.15 bays per dwelling).
Applies only when: proposal.dwelling_count >= 5.
Not met: assess against `M2.3`.
Defined terms:
- *common property* (A1 p4): has the meaning given by section 10 of the Strata Titles Act 1985.

### `C2.3.4` Visitor bays marked and signposted, on common property and connected to building entries, Perth and Peel

It should not incorporate any step, stairway, turnstile, revolving door, escalator, hazard or other impediment which would prevent it from being safely negotiated by people with disability.
