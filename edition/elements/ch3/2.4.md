## Merit assessment `M2.4`: Waste management merit assessment criteria

M2.4.1 Sufficient space for waste storage is provided that: i. is convenient for residents; ii. has collection areas which can be accessed by service vehicles; and iii. can be secured and managed where required. M2.4.2 Waste management facilities are located and screened to minimise negative impacts on the streetscape, communal areas, building entries, major openings, and the local amenity.

Source p21.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `services.waste.bin_space_per_lg_requirements`: A dedicated, accessible space holds the number and type of waste bins the local government requires for the dwelling (C2.4.1)? (yes/no)
- `services.bins_screened`: Bins screened? (yes/no)
- `services.waste.communal_storage_area_m2`: What is the waste communal storage area? (m²; communal waste storage area; above 0 triggers C2.4.3)
- `services.waste.communal_area_separated_or_screened`: The communal waste storage area is separated or screened from major openings, primary garden areas and communal open space (C2.4.3)? (yes/no)
- `proposal.dwelling_count`: What is the dwelling? (number of grouped dwellings on the site)

## Provisions

### `C2.4.1` Dedicated, accessible space for the dwelling's waste bins per local government requirements

Facts: `services.waste.bin_space_per_lg_requirements`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p21.

> C2.4.1 A dedicated and accessible space is provided to accommodate the required number and type of waste storage bins for the dwelling, in line with requirements of the local government.

Test: services.waste.bin_space_per_lg_requirements = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.4`.

### `C2.4.2` Waste storage bins screened from view

Facts: `services.bins_screened`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p21.

> C2.4.2 Waste storage bins are screened from view from communal areas, the street, public open space, and other areas accessible to the public.

Test: services.bins_screened = yes.
Not met: assess against `M2.4`.
Defined terms:
- *street* (A1 p10): any public road, communal street, private street, laneway, right-of-way or other shared access way that provides the principal frontage to a dwelling but does not include an access leg to a single battleaxe lot.
- *public open space* (A1 p8): publicly accessible land set aside for the purpose of public enjoyment and protection of unique, environmental, social and cultural values for existing and future generations. It is vested in or under the control of a public authority.

### `C2.4.3` Communal waste storage area separated or screened from major openings, primary garden areas and communal open space

Facts: `services.waste.communal_storage_area_m2`, `services.waste.communal_area_separated_or_screened`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p21.

> C2.4.3 Where a communal waste storage area is provided, it is to be separated or screened from major openings, primary garden areas and communal open space to avoid the adverse impact of potential sources of noise and odour.

Test: services.waste.communal_area_separated_or_screened = yes.
Applies only when: services.waste.communal_storage_area_m2 > 0 m².
Not met: assess against `M2.4`.
Defined terms:
- *major opening* (A1 p7): a window, door or other opening in the exterior wall of a habitable room that provides external means of light or view for that room or space, but does not include an opening or openings that: (i) in aggregate do not exceed 1m² in any such wall, (provided that adjoining or contiguous windows at the junction of two walls forming an internal angle of 90 degrees or less shall be aggregated); or (ii) are glazed in an obscure material and are not able to be opened; or (iii) have a sill height not less than 1.6m above floor level.
- *primary garden area* (A1 p8): an external ground floor area for single house and grouped dwellings set aside on a site for the exclusive use of the occupants of the dwelling to which it abuts. It may include (but is not limited to) landscaping, deep soil areas and trees, paved areas and patios with a floor level not greater than 0.5m above natural ground level.
- *communal open space* (A1 p4): outdoor areas within the lot and either at ground level or on structure that is accessible to and shared by occupants of the dwellings for communal recreational use. It does not include driveways or car parking areas.

### `C2.4.3-r1` Waste management plan for five or more grouped dwellings (Note) ⚑

Facts: `proposal.dwelling_count`.

Kind judgment; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p21.

> Note: Where five or more grouped dwellings are proposed, a waste management plan to the satisfaction of the decision-maker, is to be provided.

Test: judgment: no encoded test; assess against the text.
Applies only when: proposal.dwelling_count >= 5.
Not met: assess against `M2.4`.
