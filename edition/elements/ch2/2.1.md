## Merit assessment `M2.1`: Size and layout of dwellings merit assessment criteria

M2.1.1 Dwellings have a primary living space that: i. is proportionate to the type and size of the dwelling and intended number of occupants; ii. has a physical and visual relationship with the primary garden area, private open space and/or public open space; and iii. incorporates environmental design principles, including passive solar design and appropriate daylighting and shading, appropriate for the climate. M2.1.2 Each dwelling provides adequate, conveniently located storage for large items that are: i. proportionate to the size of the dwelling and intended number of occupants; and ii. integrated into the design of the building and/or screened from view to ensure that it is not visually intrusive when viewed from the street.

Source p15.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `proposal.primary_living_space_floor`: What is the primary living space floor? (ground / upper)
- `building.primary_living_direct_access_to_pga`: C2.1.1(i)? (yes/no)
- `building.primary_living_direct_access_to_upper_pos`: Upper-floor primary living space has direct physical and visual access to the balcony, rooftop terrace or public open space (C2.1.1(ii))? (yes/no)
- `building.upper_pos.area_m2`: What is the upper pos area? (m²; balcony or rooftop terrace serving an upper-floor primary living space (Table 2.1a))
- `building.upper_pos.min_dim_m`: What is the upper pos min dim? (m)
- `proposal.bedrooms`: What is the bedrooms? (0 for studio)
- `building.storage.area_m2`: What is the storage area? (m²; Table 2.1b storage)
- `building.storage.min_dim_m`: What is the storage min dim? (m)
- `building.storage.height_m`: What is the storage height? (m)
- `building.storage.behind_street_setback`: Storage behind street setback? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C2.1.1(i)` Primary living space on the ground floor has direct access to the primary garden area ⚑

Facts: `proposal.primary_living_space_floor`, `building.primary_living_direct_access_to_pga`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p15.

> C2.1.1 The dwelling is to have one room that is the designated primary living space where: i. the primary living space is provided on the ground floor, it is to have direct physical and visual access to the primary garden area; or

Test: building.primary_living_direct_access_to_pga = yes.
Applies only when: proposal.primary_living_space_floor == ground.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *primary living space* (A1 p8): the identified room(s) within a dwelling that is the focus of life and activity and usually the largest room. This area is connected with private open space and may include any of the following room types: living room, lounge room, family room, or an integrated open plan living area that has one of these room types together with a kitchen or dining area.
- *primary garden area* (A1 p8): an external ground floor area for single house and grouped dwellings set aside on a site for the exclusive use of the occupants of the dwelling to which it abuts. It may include (but is not limited to) landscaping, deep soil areas and trees, paved areas and patios with a floor level not greater than 0.5m above natural ground level.
⚑ Verification note: Values and wording match p15. Ambiguous on the page: the clause carries no density qualifier but tests access to the primary garden area (a term used at R50+ / R100-SL); whether it binds R40 and below is not stated.

### `C2.1.1(ii)` Primary living space on an upper floor has direct access to open space sized per Table 2.1a ⚑

Facts: `proposal.primary_living_space_floor`, `building.primary_living_direct_access_to_upper_pos`, `building.upper_pos.area_m2`, `building.upper_pos.min_dim_m`, `proposal.bedrooms`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p15 Table 2.1a.

> C2.1.1 The dwelling is to have one room that is the designated primary living space where: ii. the primary living space is provided on an upper floor, it is to have direct physical and visual access to a private open space (such as a balcony or rooftop terrace) in accordance with Table 2.1a.

Test: (building.primary_living_direct_access_to_upper_pos = yes AND building.upper_pos.area_m2 >= Table 2.1a by bedrooms AND ((proposal.bedrooms <= 1 AND building.upper_pos.min_dim_m >= 2.0 m) OR (proposal.bedrooms >= 2 AND building.upper_pos.min_dim_m >= 2.4 m))).
- Table 2.1a (Table 2.1a Private open space balcony or rooftop terrace provisions (minimum area)): bedrooms <= 1: 8 m2 (studio / 1 bedroom; minimum dimension 2 m); bedrooms 2-2: 10 m2 (printed as '2+ bedroom'; read as 2 bedrooms because the next row is '3 or more'; minimum dimension 2.4 m); bedrooms >= 3: 12 m2 (3 or more bedrooms; minimum dimension 2.4 m). ⚑ table flagged
Applies only when: proposal.primary_living_space_floor == upper.
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
Defined terms:
- *private open space* (A1 p8): means outdoor space located at ground level or on a structure that is within private ownership and provided for the recreational use of residents of the dwelling to which it abuts and excludes car parking spaces and access ways. It includes primary garden areas, outdoor living areas, balconies, courtyards and terraces.
- *balcony* (A1 p4): a balustraded platform on the outside of a dwelling with access from an upper internal room.
⚑ Verification note: Table 2.1a on p15 prints rows '2+ bedroom 10m2' and '3 or more bedrooms 12m2', which overlap; the 2.4m minimum dimension cell spans both rows. Same density-scope question as C2.1.1(i).

### `C2.1.2` Dedicated weatherproof storage area per Table 2.1b

Facts: `building.storage.area_m2`, `building.storage.min_dim_m`, `building.storage.height_m`, `building.storage.behind_street_setback`, `proposal.bedrooms`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R50+,R100-SL; applies to single_house; source p15 Table 2.1b.

> Storage (applies to R50-R80; R100-SL; LR, MR, and HR only). C2.1.2 The dwelling has exclusive use of a dedicated, weatherproof storage area in accordance with Table 2.1b.

Test: (building.storage.area_m2 >= Table 2.1b by bedrooms AND building.storage.min_dim_m >= 1.0 m AND building.storage.height_m >= 2.0 m AND building.storage.behind_street_setback = yes).
- Table 2.1b (Table 2.1b Storage provisions (minimum storage area)): bedrooms <= 1: 3 m2 (studio / 1 bedroom dwelling); bedrooms >= 2: 4 m2 (2+ bedroom dwelling).
Not met: assess against `M2.1`.
