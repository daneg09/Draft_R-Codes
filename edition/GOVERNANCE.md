# Governance

Hand-written; not generated by `python -m rcodes publish`.

## What governs

The governing documents are the draft consolidated Residential Design Codes published by the Western Australian
Planning Commission (WAPC) as PDFs: Chapter 1 Operation of the Code, Chapter 2 Single Houses, Chapter 3 Grouped
Dwellings, Chapter 4 Part 1 Apartments (R10 to R60), Chapter 4 Part 2 Apartments (R80 and above) and the
Appendices. The draft is a consultation draft, advertised 24 October 2026; submissions close 26 February 2027.

This edition is a derived, unofficial transcription of those PDFs. It is not published or endorsed by the WAPC or
the Department of Planning, Lands and Heritage. Nothing here is law, and the draft itself is not in force. Where a
record differs from the PDF, the PDF governs.

## Versions

| ID | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft-2026-10-24` | the consultation draft advertised 24 October 2026; edition 1.0 (3 November 2026) |

Versions are declared in `src/rcodes/core/versions.json`. A version ID is never reused or edited in meaning. When
the code is gazetted it will be added as a new version with its own ID (for example `gazetted-<date>`), new chapter
packages and new clause IDs. Each new record's `replaces` field will name the `draft-2026-10-24` clause it
succeeds, so the crosswalk between draft and gazetted code is data on the records. The draft records stay as they
are. (The `replaces` fields on the draft records already point to the code in force at the time the draft was
advertised; that code is not encoded here.)

## What "verified" means

Every value and text in every rule and fallback record, and every table, was checked against the rendered page
image of the draft PDF, not a text extract, in one pass run by an AI model (Claude Opus 4.5) under the author's
direction: verification pass 1, 2 November 2026, with two value corrections. The pass is recorded in each record's
stamp as `"verified": {"method": "page-image", "page": n, "date": "2026-11-02", "by": "opus-4.5-pass-1"}`; the
`by` value is the identifier of that AI pass, not a person. 123 records remain flagged where the draft page itself
is ambiguous. No human review of the records has been done yet; corrections are welcome by issue. It is a
transcription check, not a legal or professional review.

A record that still carries `"verify": true` (shown ⚑ in the digests and packs) was checked, but the page itself is
ambiguous or internally inconsistent; the record's note names the problem. These are listed in
`indexes/flagged.md`. A flag records an issue in the draft, not an unchecked value.

## Correction policy

Every change to a record after its first transcription is logged in `../CHANGELOG.md` with the record, the field,
the old and new value and the page that justified it. Wording corrections, flags cleared and draft
inconsistencies confirmed on the page are logged the same way. A change without a page is not accepted.

## How to cite a provision

Give the clause ID, the printed page and the version:

    rcodes:draft-2026-10-24/ch2/C3.3.3, draft R-Codes Chapter 2 Single Houses p31 (WAPC, advertised 24 October 2026)

Clause IDs take the form `rcodes:<version>/<chapter>/<clause>[#fragment]`, with chapters `ch1`, `ch2`, `ch3`,
`ch4p1` and `ch4p2`. A fragment (`C3.3.1#laneway`) names one encoded part of a clause. Element numbers differ
between chapters, so always give the chapter. To cite the edition as a whole, see `../CITATION.cff`.

## How to report an error

Open an issue that names the clause ID (or table ID) and the printed page, quotes what the page says, and says
what the record says instead. Errors found in the draft itself, rather than in the transcription, are recorded
as flags; they are matters for a submission to the WAPC.

## Regeneration rule

Everything in `edition/` except this file, `SKILL.md` and `worked/` is generated: `python -m rcodes publish`
writes the entry files, digests, packs, indexes, definitions and record copies; `export-jsonld` writes `graph/`;
`export-schema` writes `schema/`. The source is the records under `src/rcodes/chapters/`. Do not edit generated
files; edit the record and regenerate. `tests/test_edition.py` and `tests/test_exports.py` fail when the committed
edition, graph or schema is stale; `tests/test_worked.py` fails when the worked example's outcomes no longer match
the rules.

## Scope

Draft only. Not included: the R-Codes currently in force or an executable crosswalk to them; local instruments
(schemes, local planning policies, local development plans, structure plans) and their variations, beyond the
Chapter 1 rules on what they may vary; spatial data (lot boundaries, coding maps, Location A or B, regional
extents); plan reading, so every fact is supplied by a person or another tool.

## Disclaimer

An assessment produced with this edition, by the engine or by a model following `SKILL.md`, is not planning advice

change before gazettal. Check every finding against the published PDF and the instruments that apply to the site.
