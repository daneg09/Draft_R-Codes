## Merit assessment `M2.10`: Housing on lots less than 100m2 merit assessment criteria

M2.10.1 Housing on lots less than 100m2 are integrated with the streetscape and surrounding development and does not form the predominant housing type in a street. M2.10.2 Housing on lots less than 100m2 are designed to a high standard to contribute to the desired streetscape character and minimise adverse impacts on the amenity of adjoining properties.

Source p24.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `site.area_m2`: What is the area? (m²; site area)

Test: building.fronts_street_or_row = yes.
Applies only when: site.area_m2 < 100.0 m².
Not met: assess against `M2.10`.
Defined terms:
- *primary street* (A1 p8): unless otherwise designated by the local government, the sole or principal public road that provides access to the major entry (front door) to the dwelling or building.
- *secondary street* (A1 p9): in the case of a site that has access from more than one public road, a road that is not the primary street.
- *right-of-way* (A1 p8): a strip of land such as a laneway, available either for use by the general public, or a restricted section of the community, and may be created by subdivision, specific transfer, or continued use over a period of years. They may be: (i) private, where the land is created in a plan of subdivision and available to landowners that are legally entitled; or (ii) public, where the land has been vested in the Crown.
⚑ Verification note: Ambiguous on the page: the element sub-heading (p24) reads 'Housing on lots less than 100m2 – applies to single houses and grouped dwellings in areas coded R100-SL only', while C2.10.1 names only the R100-SL coding; whether an R100-SL lot of 100m2 or more is caught is not stated.

### `C2.10.1(ii)` R100-SL dwelling: all R-Codes provisions apply subject to the Table 2.10a modifications ⚑

Facts: none (judgment against the text).

Kind judgment; column C; coding R100-SL; applies to single_house; source p25 Table 2.10a.

> Housing on lots less than 100m2 – applies to single houses and grouped dwellings in areas coded R100-SL only. C2.10.1 Single houses in areas coded R100-SL are to comply with the following: ii. all provisions of the R-Codes to apply, subject to the modifications in Table 2.10a.

Test: judgment: no encoded test; assess against the text.
Applies only when: site.area_m2 < 100.0 m².
Not met: assess against `M2.10`.
Defined terms:
- *primary garden area* (A1 p8): an external ground floor area for single house and grouped dwellings set aside on a site for the exclusive use of the occupants of the dwelling to which it abuts. It may include (but is not limited to) landscaping, deep soil areas and trees, paved areas and patios with a floor level not greater than 0.5m above natural ground level.
- *boundary wall* (A1 p4): a wall, on or less than 600mm from any lot boundary (green title or survey-strata lot), other than a street boundary.
- *street setback* (A1 p10): the horizontal distance between the street boundary and a building, measured at right angles (90 degrees) to the street boundary.
