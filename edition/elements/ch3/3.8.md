## Merit assessment `M3.8`: Retaining existing dwellings merit assessment criteria

M3.8.1 Retained dwellings, dwelling extension or redevelopment are to: i. enhance the streetscape appearance of the existing dwelling(s) retained; or ii. complement established or future built form in the locality as specified within the relevant local planning framework. M3.8.2 Retained dwellings provide adequate outdoor and indoor amenity for residents.

Source p43.
