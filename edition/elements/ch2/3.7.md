## Merit assessment `M3.7`: Access merit assessment criteria

M3.7.1 Access for each development site is to: i. balance pedestrian and cyclist safety while providing safe vehicle access; ii. minimise the extent of impervious surfaces; iii. provide legible access; and iv. include high quality landscaping features. M3.7.2 Vehicle access is designed and located to: i. minimise the number and width of vehicle access points and the impact on the streetscape; ii. provide access to the street with the lowest volume of traffic; and iii. accommodate sloping sites and retaining walls. M3.7.3 The width of the battleaxe leg may be reduced where it is necessary to retain an existing dwelling provided: i. safe vehicle access, setbacks or clearances and sightlines are achieved; and ii. it is only reduced for the portion required to retain the dwelling. P3.7.4 Unobstructed sightlines provided at vehicle access points to ensure safety and visibility along vehicle access ways, streets, rights-of-way, communal streets, crossovers, and footpaths. P3.7.5 Legible, safe, and direct access for residents and their visitors to move between communal car parking areas or public streets and individual dwellings. P3.7.6 Development with potential to be subdivided to create 20 or more green title lots, strata lots or survey-strata lots provides legible internal connections and access to streets and be designed to accommodate traffic movement and volume, visitor parking, pedestrian access, street shade trees, utility services and access for waste collection and emergency service vehicles.

Source p39.

## Provisions

### `C3.7.1` Vehicle access from the lowest-order available street

Facts: `parking.access.from_lowest_order_street`, `site.has_communal_street`, `site.has_row`, `site.has_secondary_street`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p39.

> Where multiple accesses are available, vehicle access to on site car parking spaces to be provided via the lowest available street in the hierarchy, as follows: i. where available, from a right-of-way or communal street available for lawful use to access the relevant site and which is trafficable and drained from the property boundary to a constructed street; or ii. from the secondary street or primary street where no right-of-way or communal street exists.

Test: parking.access.from_lowest_order_street = yes.
Applies only when: (site.has_row = yes OR site.has_communal_street = yes OR site.has_secondary_street = yes).
Not met: assess against `M3.7`.
Defined terms:
- *right-of-way* (A1 p8): a strip of land such as a laneway, available either for use by the general public, or a restricted section of the community, and may be created by subdivision, specific transfer, or continued use over a period of years. They may be: (i) private, where the land is created in a plan of subdivision and available to landowners that are legally entitled; or (ii) public, where the land has been vested in the Crown.
- *communal street* (A1 p4): common property or private street providing joint access to two or more dwellings in a residential development and may include vehicle and pedestrian access, and landscaping.
- *secondary street* (A1 p9): in the case of a site that has access from more than one public road, a road that is not the primary street.
- *primary street* (A1 p8): unless otherwise designated by the local government, the sole or principal public road that provides access to the major entry (front door) to the dwelling or building.

### `C3.7.2` One vehicle access point per lot (R40 and above) ⚑

Facts: `parking.access.count`, `proposal.retained_dwelling`, `site.frontage_m`, `site.has_communal_street`, `site.has_row`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R40+; applies to single_house; source p39.

> In areas R40 and above, vehicle access points are limited to one per lot (refer Figure 3.7a) except where: i. an existing dwelling is being retained that has an established access point that is not able to serve the other dwellings; ii. dwellings front the street and access is not available from a communal street or rear right-of-way; or iii. the lot frontage exceeds 40m, two vehicle access points are permitted.

Test: parking.access.count <= 1.
Exception 1: (i) retained dwelling with an established access point. When proposal.retained_dwelling = yes: the provision does not apply. ⚑
Exception 2: (ii) dwellings front the street and no communal street or rear ROW access. When (site.has_row = no AND site.has_communal_street = no): the provision does not apply. ⚑
Exception 3: (iii) lot frontage exceeds 40 m: two access points. When site.frontage_m > 40 m: sets value = 2.
Not met: assess against `M3.7`.
Defined terms:
- *frontage* (A1 p5): the width of a lot at the primary street setback line, provided that in the case of battleaxe or other irregularly shaped lots, it shall be as determined by the decision-maker.
⚑ Verification note: Wording, R40 and above coding and the 40m / two access points limb confirmed. Exceptions (i) and (ii) state no effect and are written for several dwellings ('serve the other dwellings', 'dwellings front the street'); how they apply to a single house is not stated on the page. Encoded as not applicable.

### `C3.7.4` Exit to the street in forward gear

### `C3.7.8(i)` Pedestrian access leg minimum width 1.5 m

Facts: `proposal.retained_dwelling`, `site.has_pedestrian_access_leg`, `site.pedestrian_access_leg_width_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p40.

> Where a pedestrian access leg is required to provide access from a dwelling site to a public street, it is to: i. be a minimum width of 1.5m; and ii. provide a continuous path of travel with a minimum width of 1m, clear of any utilities or minor projections (excluding eaves). The pedestrian access leg may be reduced to 1m where required to retain an existing dwelling.

Test: site.pedestrian_access_leg_width_m >= 1.5 m.
Applies only when: site.has_pedestrian_access_leg = yes.
Exception 1: reduced to 1 m where required to retain an existing dwelling. When proposal.retained_dwelling = yes: sets value = 1.0. ⚑
Not met: assess against `M3.7`.
Defined terms:
- *pedestrian access leg* (A1 p8): provides access from a public street to a dwelling where sole vehicular access is via a rear right-of-way. It can be in the form of a portion of the rear lot or as common property in the case of a strata title scheme.

### `C3.7.8(ii)` Pedestrian access leg continuous 1 m clear path

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p40.

> Where a pedestrian access leg is required to provide access from a dwelling site to a public street, it is to: ii. provide a continuous path of travel with a minimum width of 1m, clear of any utilities or minor projections (excluding eaves).

Test: site.pedestrian_access_leg_clear_path_m >= 1.0 m.
Applies only when: site.has_pedestrian_access_leg = yes.
