## Merit assessment `M1.3`: Water management and conservation merit assessment criteria

M1.3.1 Flooding risk is reduced to limit the impact of major rainfall events. M1.3.2 Stormwater is managed on site wherever possible either by containment or infiltration, as permitted by the soil and other site conditions, or otherwise appropriately treated to reduce the export of nutrients, sediments and other pollutants, prior to off site discharge. M1.3.3 Development incorporates water sensitive urban design mechanisms, including water conservation approaches and site appropriate stormwater collection, retention, treatment and reuse.

Source p13.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `landscape.stormwater_retained_on_site`: Runoff from a small rainfall event retained on site (C1.3.1)? (yes/no)
- `landscape.stormwater_to_system_required`: Decision-maker requires connection (C1.3.2)? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C1.3.1` Stormwater from a small rainfall event retained on site

Facts: `landscape.stormwater_retained_on_site`, `landscape.stormwater_to_system_required`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p13.

> C1.3.1 Stormwater runoff draining from roofs, driveways, communal streets and other impervious surfaces generated by a small rainfall event to be retained on site, with run-off directed to garden areas, rainwater tanks and infiltration cells (e.g. soakwells), appropriate to climatic, local soil and groundwater conditions. C1.3.2 Notwithstanding C1.3.1, stormwater may be directed to a district or local stormwater drainage system where required by the decision-maker due to climatic, local soil or groundwater conditions.

Test: landscape.stormwater_retained_on_site = yes.
Exception 1: C1.3.2 directed to a district or local stormwater drainage system where required by the decision-maker. When landscape.stormwater_to_system_required = yes: the provision does not apply. (C1.3.2 (register ch2-014))
Not met: assess against `M1.3`.
Defined terms:
- *small rainfall event* (A1 p9): the first 15mm of a rainfall event (refer Department of Water and Environmental Regulation).
