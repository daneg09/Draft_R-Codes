## Merit assessment `M3.2`: Building height merit assessment criteria

M3.2.1 Building height, bulk and scale is appropriate for the existing and/or desired future streetscape and local character of the area and nearby development. M3.2.2 Building height is considerate of the impact on the amenity of adjoining properties or the streetscape, including road reserves and public open space reserves, and where appropriate maintains: i. adequate solar access into indoor and outdoor active habitable space and solar collectors; and ii. access to views of significance.

Source p28.

## Provisions

### `C3.2.1#height` Maximum total building height

Facts: `building.total_height_m`, `site.r_code`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to single_house; source p28 Table 3.2a.

> C3.2.1 Building height complies with Table 3.2a. Table 3.2a Maximum building heights, maximum total building height: R2-R35 10m; R40-R60 13m; R80, R100, R160 and LR, MR and HR 16m. Refer Figure 3.2a for building height and natural ground level measurement guidance. Refer Figure 3.2b for total building height guidance. This table provides a maximum building height only and development will need to consider other elements such as 3.9 Solar access for adjoining sites.

Test: building.total_height_m <= Table 3.2a-height by coding.
- Table 3.2a-height (Table 3.2a Maximum building heights: maximum total building height): R2-R35: 10 m; R40-R60: 13 m; R80+/LR/MR/HR: 16 m (R80, R100, R160 and LR, MR and HR); R100-SL: 16 m (Not listed in Table 3.2a; inferred from Table 2.10a 'maximum four storey building height in accordance with Table 3.2a' and Table 2A).
Not met: assess against `M3.2`.
Defined terms:
- *natural ground level* (A1 p7): the levels on a site which precede the proposed development, excluding any site works unless approved by the decision-maker or established as part of subdivision of the land preceding development.

### `C3.2.1#storeys` Maximum number of storeys

Facts: `proposal.storeys`, `site.r_code`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding numeric,LR,MR,HR; applies to single_house; source p28 Table 3.2a.

> C3.2.1 Building height complies with Table 3.2a. Table 3.2a Maximum building heights, maximum number of storeys: R2-R35 2; R40-R60 3; R80, R100, R160 and LR, MR and HR 4.

Test: proposal.storeys <= Table 3.2a-storeys by coding.
- Table 3.2a-storeys (Table 3.2a Maximum building heights: maximum number of storeys): R2-R35: 2 storeys; R40-R60: 3 storeys; R80+/LR/MR/HR: 4 storeys (R80, R100, R160 and LR, MR and HR).
Not met: assess against `M3.2`.
Defined terms:
- *storey* (A1 p10): the portion of a building which is situated between the top of any floor and the top of the floor next above it and if there is no floor above it, that portion between the top of the floor and the ceiling above it but does not include: (i) a basement; (ii) a space that contains only a lift shaft, stairway or meter room; (iii) roof top terrace with no permanent impervious roof structure; (iv) a mezzanine; or (v) a loft. Double height floors greater than 5m floor to ceiling are counted as two floors.
- *height, building* (A1 p6): this is the distance between the point where the base of the wall meets the natural ground level and measured to the highest point of a wall or roof of a building vertically above that point excluding minor projections.
