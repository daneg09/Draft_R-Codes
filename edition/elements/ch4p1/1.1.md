## Merit assessment `M1.1`: Private open space merit assessment criteria

M1.1.1 Dwellings are designed to have direct access to private open space which provides for entertaining, leisure and connection to the outdoors that is: i. of sufficient size and dimension to be functional and usable for the intended number of dwelling occupants; ii. is sited, oriented and designed for occupant amenity, including consideration of solar access and natural ventilation appropriate to the climatic region; and iii. capable of use in conjunction with a primary living space of the dwelling. M1.1.2 Private open space allows for sufficient uncovered area to: i. permit winter sun and natural ventilation into the dwelling; and ii. provide for soft landscaping, including the planting of a tree(s) and deep soil area. M1.1.3 Balconies balance the need for outlook, solar access and natural ventilation with: i. visual privacy considerations; ii. acoustic and noise impacts; and iii. local climatic considerations such as high winds. M1.1.4 Increasing the area of communal open space commensurate with a decrease in private open space may be appropriate where there is an explicit intent to facilitate communal living and it can be demonstrated that the communal open space: i. is of high amenity and provided with quality landscaping; ii. is easily accessible and equitable for all dwellings within the development; and iii. meets the needs of the occupants and provides opportunities for social interaction.

Source p9.

- `dwellings.min.pos_area_m2`: What is the min pos area? (m²; smallest private open space (balcony, terrace or courtyard) of any apartment (Table 1.1a))
- `dwellings.min.pos_dwelling_category`: What is the min pos dwelling category? (studio_1bed / 2bed / 3bed_plus / ground_floor; Table 1.1a size category of the apartment that dwellings.min.pos_area_m2 and pos_min_dim_m describe; a ground floor dwelling is ground_floor whatever its bedrooms (C1.1.1 to C1.1.1-r3))
- `site.r_code`: What is the r code? (code; R-coding of the lot)
- `dwellings.min.pos_min_dim_m`: What is the min pos min dim? (m)
- `dwellings.balconies_provided`: Any apartment has a balcony (C1.1.2 condition)? (yes/no)
- `dwellings.min.balcony_unscreened_perimeter_pct`: What is the min balcony unscreened perimeter? (%; lowest % of balcony perimeter unscreened (C1.1.2))

## Provisions

### `C1.1.1` Private open space per apartment: minimum area (Table 1.1a), R30 to R60

Facts: `dwellings.min.pos_area_m2`, `dwellings.min.pos_dwelling_category`, `site.r_code`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding R30-R60; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p9 Table 1.1a.

> Primary garden area. C1.1.1 A minimum of one private open space area is provided for the exclusive use of each dwelling, in accordance with Table 1.1a. Table 1.1a Primary garden area provisions. Minimum private open space area (m2 per dwelling): Studio/1 bedroom 8m2; 2 bedrooms 10m2; 3 or more bedrooms 12m2; Ground floor dwelling 15m2. When calculating the extent of private open space for apartments, exclude service areas such as bin storage, clothes drying, air conditioning units and the like.

Test: dwellings.min.pos_area_m2 >= Table 1.1a by coding,pos_dwelling_category.
- Table 1.1a (Table 1.1a Private open space per apartment: minimum area by dwelling size): R30-R60, pos_dwelling_category studio_1bed: 8 m2; R30-R60, pos_dwelling_category 2bed: 10 m2; R30-R60, pos_dwelling_category 3bed_plus: 12 m2; R30-R60, pos_dwelling_category ground_floor: 15 m2.
Not met: assess against `M1.1`.
Defined terms:
- *private open space* (A1 p8): means outdoor space located at ground level or on a structure that is within private ownership and provided for the recreational use of residents of the dwelling to which it abuts and excludes car parking spaces and access ways. It includes primary garden areas, outdoor living areas, balconies, courtyards and terraces.
- *primary living space* (A1 p8): the identified room(s) within a dwelling that is the focus of life and activity and usually the largest room. This area is connected with private open space and may include any of the following room types: living room, lounge room, family room, or an integrated open plan living area that has one of these room types together with a kitchen or dining area.

### `C1.1.2` Balconies unscreened for at least 25% of the total balcony perimeter

Facts: `dwellings.balconies_provided`, `dwellings.min.balcony_unscreened_perimeter_pct`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to multiple_dwelling; source p9.

> C1.1.2 Balconies are to be unscreened for at least 25% of the total perimeter of the balcony (refer Figure 1.1a). Figure 1.1a note: Boundary perimeter length = A + B + C + D. Minimum 25% of balcony perimeter to be unscreened. Total balcony perimeter to include sides that abut building walls.

Test: dwellings.min.balcony_unscreened_perimeter_pct >= 25%.
Applies only when: dwellings.balconies_provided = yes.
Not met: assess against `M1.1`.
Defined terms:
- *balcony* (A1 p4): a balustraded platform on the outside of a dwelling with access from an upper internal room.
