## Merit assessment `M3.5`: Site works and retaining walls merit assessment criteria

M3.5.1 Development that considers and responds to the natural features of the site and requires minimal excavation/fill. M3.5.2 Where excavation/fill is necessary, all finished levels respect the natural ground level at the lot boundary of the site and as viewed from the street. M3.5.3 Retaining walls that result in land which can be effectively used for the benefit of residents, do not detrimentally affect the amenity of adjoining properties in the opinion of the decision-maker, and are designed, engineered and landscaped having due regard to the provisions of element 3.10 Visual privacy.

Source p37.

## Questions to ask the applicant

- `site_works.forward_of_setback_for_access_drainage_light`: The change is for access, drainage or natural light? (yes/no)
- `site_works.forward_of_setback_max_change_m`: What is the forward of setback max change? (m; max fill or excavation forward of the street setback line)
- `site_works.retaining_max_height_m`: What is the retaining max height? (m; retaining wall or fill height behind the setback line)
- `site_works.retaining_setback_m`: What is the retaining setback? (m; its setback from the lot boundary)
- `site_works.excavation_behind_setback`: Excavation behind setback? (yes/no)

## Provisions

### `C3.5.1` Site works forward of the street setback line

Facts: `site_works.forward_of_setback_for_access_drainage_light`, `site_works.forward_of_setback_max_change_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p37.

> Retaining walls, fill and excavation forward of the street setback line, not more than 0.5m above or below the natural ground level, except where necessary to provide for pedestrian universal access and/or vehicle access, drainage works, or natural light to a dwelling.

Test: site_works.forward_of_setback_max_change_m <= 0.5 m.
Exception 1: necessary for universal or vehicle access, drainage or natural light. When site_works.forward_of_setback_for_access_drainage_light = yes: the provision does not apply.
Not met: assess against `M3.5`.
Defined terms:
- *street setback line* (A1 p10): the minimum distance between the street boundary and a building in accordance with Part B, Table B and Part C, Table 3.3a.
- *natural ground level* (A1 p7): the levels on a site which precede the proposed development, excluding any site works unless approved by the decision-maker or established as part of subdivision of the land preceding development.

### `C3.5.2` Retaining walls and fill behind the street setback: setback per Table 3.5a

Facts: `site_works.retaining_max_height_m`, `site_works.retaining_setback_m`.

Kind measurable; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p37 Table 3.5a.

> Retaining walls and fill within the site and behind the street setback to comply with Table 3.5a.

Test: site_works.retaining_setback_m >= Table 3.5a by retaining_max_height_m.
- Table 3.5a (Table 3.5a Setback of retaining walls and fill): retaining_max_height_m <= 1.0: 0 m (1m or less); retaining_max_height_m <= 1.5: 1.5 m; retaining_max_height_m <= 2.0: 2.0 m; retaining_max_height_m <= 2.5: 2.5 m; retaining_max_height_m >= 2.5: 3.0 m (3m +).
Not met: assess against `M3.5`.
Defined terms:
- *lot boundary* (A1 p7): the boundary between a lot and any other parcel of land, excluding a street boundary.

### `C3.5.3` Excavation behind the street setback line may extend to the lot boundary

Facts: `site_works.excavation_behind_setback`.

Kind conditional; column C; coding all; applies to grouped_dwelling; source p37.

> Excavation within the site is permitted behind the street setback line and may be constructed up to the lot boundary.

Test: site_works.excavation_behind_setback = yes.
Not met: assess against `M3.5`.
